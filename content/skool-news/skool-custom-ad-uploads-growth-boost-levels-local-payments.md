# Skool Lets You Upload Your Own Ads: Growth Boost Levels, Freemium Limits and Local Payments

Skool News #79 confirms that the Growth tab and AI-generated ads are now live for everyone, previews the ability for higher-level groups to upload their own ad creatives, and gives a roadmap update that includes a rebuilt payment system designed to cut card declines worldwide.

## The Growth tab and AI ads are live

Every group now has a Growth tab in its settings. It shows which Growth Boost level the group is on (1 through 4), what each level unlocks, and the requirements to reach and keep a level. Below that, owners can see the ads Skool generates for them automatically with AI, built from the group's cover image, icon, profile picture and about page.

Not everyone liked their ads, so Skool quickly shipped two options. You can change the inputs (cover, icon, profile picture, about page) and the ads regenerate, which can take up to an hour. Or you can switch AI ads off entirely, in which case the ads disappear immediately and Skool simply uses your cover image. The hosts said most owners like their ads, with exceptions like a tattoo artist whose AI ads showed tattoos they never did, or a math teacher worried about invented equations.

## Why free and freemium groups don't level up

A recurring question came from free and freemium groups. Reaching level 2 requires around 100 about page visitors and at least one new paying member, so a group with no paying customers can't get there, and the same logic applies to levels 3 and 4, which count paying sign-ups over the last 30 days. Skool says it tested freemium extensively and it simply doesn't work with ads: even 7-day free trials are hard to model, while a free member might convert months later or never. For now, freemium groups get no AI creatives and only light promotion in Growth Boost. Skool's own analysis found that people who start free rarely become paying, while people who start paid tend to join more paid groups.

## Custom ad uploads are coming

AI ads are aimed at busy owners or people who hate making ads. For creative owners, Skool is adding full control. Level 3 groups will soon be able to upload custom image ads, starting with around six slots next to the AI ones and growing toward 30. Custom and AI ads will compete, and whichever performs better becomes the default. Level 4 adds partnership ads, your own social handle, and uploaded video ads, with spend of up to $10,000 a day and no real cap on the number of video ads. Level 2 starts with about 10 AI images. If you see fewer ads than the number shown on your level, that's expected: Skool is still working up to those counts.

Skool also plans an "outlier" ad library showing the best-performing ads across Skool communities, with a threshold of roughly five times the average, so owners can find inspiration before making their own.

## Roadmap: Live Calls V2, local payments, native app

- **Live Calls V2:** a full rebuild of calls, webinars and recordings for less lag, fewer audio glitches and higher quality, possibly within a week or two.
- **Payment methods:** described as the biggest change Skool has made. Skool is setting up local entities and merchant accounts in regions like Europe and Latin America so transactions run domestically, raising authorization rates and enabling local methods such as PayPal, Klarna or Alipay in one adaptive checkout. Declined payments will be retried on up to three fallback processors. Multiple currencies come after this layer. Skool expects higher conversion and lower churn from fewer declines.
- **Up next:** global search for users and groups, a global calendar, and a faster native app, already in internal prototype.

## Why it matters

The level requirements make one thing explicit: Skool's growth engine is built around paid communities with real paying sign-ups. For anyone evaluating groups, that means paid communities that keep adding members are the ones getting the most platform traffic, which is exactly the kind of momentum worth tracking over time.
