# Skool Growth Boost Levels Explained, Plus Reviews in Discovery and a Redesigned Discovery Tab

Skool News #76 covers several product updates: review ratings across Discovery and profiles, a simplified Discovery tab without keywords, about page and invite tab cleanups, and a clear breakdown of the four Growth Boost levels.

## Reviews now show in Discovery and on profiles

Star ratings and review counts now appear on Discovery listings and on user profiles, in addition to Google search results.

## Discovery tab redesign: keywords are gone

The Discovery settings tab has been redesigned, and the keyword field, which allowed up to 11 keywords, has been removed. Most groups' keywords already appeared on their about page, so the field added little and confused people. To rank in search, just make sure the words people would search for appear on your about page.

The tab now explains Skool's network: about 35 million users spending a billion dollars a year, reached through three surfaces:

- **Discovery search**
- **Suggested communities**, which are getting smarter and will be personalized next
- **Off-site ads**, currently on Facebook and Instagram, with TikTok and probably AppLovin next, and other major ad networks later

The Growth Boost slider is now prominent, and your bid plus efficiency score affect all three surfaces.

## About page tips and invite tab cleanup

New owners didn't always realize the about page is their landing page and checkout. It now prompts owners to show what's inside and why people should join, makes the view-as-member tool easier to find, and links to an example about page from a successful group.

The invite tab caused support problems when owners uploaded CSVs into paid groups and members got in without paying. It now leads with sharing your Skool link: put it in your bio, send people to the about page, then check your dashboard to see which sources work. Email and CSV invites are still available behind a warning.

## The four Growth Boost levels

1. **Level 1, cover image in carousel:** the default for groups with little data or weak stats, as long as there are signs of life. A new system, due within about a week, adapts the cover image to each ad placement instead of cropping it badly.
2. **Level 2, 10 custom creatives:** for okay stats, roughly 100 about page visitors and one signup in the last 30 days. Skool generates dedicated ads from your cover image, keeping your style and testing angles like calling out the audience. The hosts recommend having your face on the cover image and a clear audience. A weak about page can stop the system from generating ads at all.
3. **Level 3, partnership ads with dedicated static campaigns:** for good efficiency. Owners connect Instagram and Facebook to run co-branded ads, with spend around $500+ a day and possibly up to about $2,000.
4. **Level 4, partnership ads with dedicated video campaigns:** for the most efficient groups, which can upload their own video ads. Skool has run about 10 of these manually.

The hosts expect this to roll out in one to two weeks and work at least twice as well.

## Growth Boost wins

They shared examples. One creator with 5 million YouTube subscribers got more new members in three days than in the previous month. Another creator, already making about $85,000 a month, saw a 7x day within 48 hours and $35,000 in new MRR over 30 days, and said Growth Boost carried his business while he couldn't make content.

A Latin dance academy at $49 a month makes about $30,000 to $31,000 a month, a $48 a month health and energy group makes about $17,000, and Bulletproof Cycling makes about $41,000 with a very simple about page. Separately, a passionate Skool member has started a year-long US bus tour of meetups.
