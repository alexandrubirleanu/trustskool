# How Ym Babie Built a $170,000/Month Trading Skool From Instagram

Jade, known online as Ym Babie, runs Trading Bootcamp, a day trading community on Skool. The hosts found her through an analysis of Skool owners growing from Instagram, where she ranked as the most efficient at turning followers into revenue. During the call she put her MRR at about $155K, with 88% engagement and 77% retention.

## From a private Instagram page to Skool

Jade has traded for about 10 years and taught for six or seven. She started by charging for access to a private Instagram page, reaching 10,000 members in the first year with no products at all. Later she sold an eBook online, but had around five merchant accounts blocked in a row, with $30,000 or more held in each. That pushed her to Skool.

At first, buying the eBook for $500 granted access to the community. When she moved her existing members over from Instagram, she says 1,500 joined in the first hour.

## Launch with almost nothing

On day one the community held the eBook and her. Skool started as a meeting place to replace capped Instagram group chats, then added weekly Zoom calls and a weekly watch list that members repurchased each week. She ran on one module and one eBook for about a year and a half before building out the classroom, which now has nine modules, around 40 videos and recorded live trading sessions. New content came from members asking for it.

## The Instagram funnel

Her funnel is simple: Instagram content, a Skool link in her bio, and a ManyChat automation that sends the link.

- **The ManyChat message** answers the questions she gets most often in DMs. She added a follow-up offering an eBook to people who don't click, but says almost nobody takes it.
- **Reels** ride trending sounds and formats, usually around 10 seconds, with a call to action in the caption. Her best performer has about 405K views. She posts with no schedule and puts little planning into them.
- **Content sells the outcome**, not the contents. She shows lifestyle and results rather than listing modules, and targets three types of followers: people interested in the skill, in her personality, and in her lifestyle.
- **Stories** go out daily, built from member wins and reviews on a template, always with the community link.
- **She treats Instagram as business only.** Dropping personal posts cost her followers and views, but she says revenue went up.

She keeps live sessions and DM access exclusive to paying members. Her about page was very basic until recently, because visitors arrive already sold.

## TikTok Lives and conversion rate

She started going live on TikTok two weeks before the call and had already picked up about 20 members, saying TikTok pushes lives to new viewers more than Instagram does. On conversion, she shared that her about page converts at only 1.7%, but on about 30,000 views. Her focus is traffic, not conversion rate, and she has never run paid ads herself.

## Pricing and free trials

She says there was no real pricing strategy. The community went from $500 a year to $1,500 a year, and now uses three tiers at $50, $100 and $200 a month. She raised prices as she added value like daily live trading. She tried a weekend free trial and got roughly 300 to 350 signups, but only about 40 converted, so she prefers the $50 tier as the entry point.

## Daily routine and retention

She goes live every weekday at 9 a.m. to review news and trade, usually finishing by 10:30 or 11, then answers posts and DMs. Extras include tutoring sessions every other Tuesday, one-on-ones, trading games, giveaways, challenges and in-person meetups.

## Why it matters

Trading Bootcamp is a reminder that an owner showing up daily, and a culture members feel part of, can matter more than a polished about page. Her final advice was simply to launch.
