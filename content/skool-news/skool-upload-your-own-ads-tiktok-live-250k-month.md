# Skool Lets You Upload Your Own Ads, Plus How Samuel G Hit $250K/Month With TikTok Live

Skool News #80 announces that groups at Growth Boost level 3 or 4 can now upload their own image ads, then shares lessons from a meetup of Platinum creators, including how Samuel G grew a $25 a month community with TikTok Live and no existing audience.

## Upload your own image ads

In the Growth tab of group settings, level 3 and level 4 groups now see slots to upload their own ads next to the AI-generated ones. Images should be 1080x1080, and there are six custom slots. Owners can run both sets together or switch the generated ads off and run only their own.

The hosts recommend keeping both on for most people, because Skool will split test its generated ads against yours anyway. If you really dislike the generated ones, you can turn them off or regenerate them. Some details are still settling: level 3 currently shows eight generated ads, and the plan is to trim that to six so it is six versus six, then add more formats and more upload slots. The hosts expect level 3 to eventually have around 30 ads, while level 4 has no limit and could run 100 video ads. Level 2 gets the generated ads only.

## Lessons from Skool Platinum

Platinum is the badge for communities at $100,000 in monthly recurring revenue. Around 30 Platinum creators spent a day at Skool sharing what works. Three takeaways came out of it.

**TikTok Live works without an audience.** Samuel G runs Creative Owners Club, a $25 a month community about buying a house without a bank, with almost 12,000 members. He went from zero to over $200,000 a month in a few months with no followers on other platforms and no ads, just by going live on TikTok every day for a couple of hours. The hosts explain that TikTok's system listens to what you talk about and sends interested viewers into your live, even if you start by answering imagined questions to an empty room. He has taught the method to his affiliates and to other Skool creators, and for some of them it is now their top traffic source. His interview, plus a recording of one of his real lives, is in the Platinum Interviews section of the Skoolers classroom.

**Keep the business simple.** Most Platinum creators have one community and one main traffic source.

**Traffic is the job.** You do not make much money without a lot of traffic, so you need to nail one traffic source and one product.

## Roadmap update

- **Live Calls V2:** rebuilt and going to some hosts with large calls for real-world testing, with a wider release possibly the following week.
- **Growth Boost V2:** more ad formats are coming. The hosts say they are not even at 1 or 2% of its potential.
- **Payment methods**, a secret project, then **global search and a global calendar**. Search will rank people you are closest to first.
- **Currencies** and a **native app** after that.

## Skool of the week

James Lu's SAT prep community charges $99 a month and makes about $102,000 a month. He told the hosts his conversion rate rose after featuring a top-scoring student on his about page. Megan Robinson's makeup community makes about $9,400 a month at $19 a month with a free trial. A third group went from zero to more than $100,000 in three days by driving about 15,000 people to a YouTube live and moving them into a premium community.

## Why this matters for people evaluating Skool communities

Custom ad uploads give established owners more control over how Skool promotes them, but the bigger lesson is the Platinum one: the top communities pair one clear offer with one reliable traffic source. When sizing up a group, consistent traffic and a simple offer tend to say more than a long feature list.
