# Skool Growth Boost V2: 200,000+ Custom Ads, Partnership Ads and a New Growth Tab

Skool News #77 introduces a Growth tab in group settings, explains how Growth Boost levels work, and announces what the hosts call the biggest single change to Growth Boost so far: more than 200,000 custom ad creatives that refresh automatically when a group changes.

## The Growth tab and partnership ads

Group owners now have a Growth tab in their settings. Once a group meets the listed requirements, a lock disappears and the owner can connect their social handle to run partnership ads, the co-branded format that shows a creator's name alongside Skool. The level above that lets owners upload their own video ads. The feature is already live for a number of groups and is being scaled up.

The most common question from owners was how close they are to unlocking it. Skool's answer is a planned progression view showing your current Growth Boost level, what you get at that level, and which actions move you to the next one. The hosts call this a high priority.

## How the levels were described

- **Level 1:** your cover image appears in Skool's ad carousel. Cover images are being re-cropped for every group so they no longer look cut off.
- **Level 2:** about 10 custom creatives generated for your group, each pushing a different angle such as your audience's problems or your proof. To qualify you need roughly 100 clicks to your about page and at least one paid sign-up in the last 30 days.
- **Level 3:** partnership ads with their own dedicated campaign and budget, which the hosts say can reach a few thousand dollars a day.
- **Level 4:** uploaded video ads. The most spent on a single group so far is about $5,000 a day, around $150,000 a month, and the hosts expect that ceiling to rise.

## Why 200,000 creatives matters

The earlier Growth Boost setup had several problems: cover images cropped badly, creative generation was slow and could not reach every qualifying group, and ads went stale. When creatives fatigued they were not refreshed, and if an owner changed their price or packaging the running ads fell out of sync.

The new system generates custom ads for every group that qualifies and can create or update them across the platform within a couple of hours. Changing your price, cover image or positioning now triggers new creatives within about an hour. The ads borrow the style, fonts and colors of your cover image, and the hosts are clear that Skool will not invent a look from scratch. Good packaging, meaning a strong cover image and about page, is still essential.

## Roadmap snapshot

- **Live Calls V2:** a full rebuild of live streaming, in internal testing, aiming for Zoom and Google Meet level stability, with better performance on weak connections, a better chat without the old character limit, and sharper screen sharing for things like trading charts.
- **Payment methods:** in progress, with options like PayPal, Pix and Klarna mentioned.
- **A secret project**, plus global search, a global calendar, currencies and a fully native app planned next.

## Skool of the week

Featured groups included Learn French with Stories, at about $16,600 a month on a $39 price, and a window cleaning business accelerator charging around $2,000 a month that makes about $111,000 a month with 132 members.

## Why this matters for people evaluating Skool communities

The hosts end on a point worth keeping in mind: Growth Boost is a tailwind, not a substitute for effort. It reads a group's stats, such as about page traffic and paid sign-ups, and assigns a level from there. A group that is doing nothing will not get boosted. For anyone comparing communities, steady organic traffic and real paid sign-ups are what unlock Skool's own promotion, so they also say a lot about how a group is likely to grow.
