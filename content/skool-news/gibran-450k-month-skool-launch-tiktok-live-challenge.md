# Gibran's $450K/Month Skool Launch, Live Calls V2 Fixes and a Free TikTok Live Challenge

Skool News #83 covers bug fixes for the newly shipped Live Calls V2, breaks down what the hosts call the biggest launch they have seen on Skool, and opens applications for a free 30-day TikTok Live challenge run with Samuel G.

## Live Calls V2: the first week of fixes

Live Calls V2 shipped the previous week, and some bugs appeared once it was used at scale, which the hosts say they expected despite internal testing. The main one: on call formats where many people had video on in a grid, the call lagged, and so did the recording. Most of those critical bugs are now fixed, so large grid calls with cameras on, and their recordings, should work well.

Smaller issues remain, such as moving someone into the grid when they start speaking so viewers can see who is talking. The hosts expect things to be in a good place by the end of the week. Skool asks for a five-star rating at the end of every live call, and the new version matched the old version's score straight away, even though the old one had months of improvements behind it.

## Gibran's Creator Pathway: $100K to $450K MRR in one launch

Gibran runs Creator Pathway, a community about turning content into income with a focus on TikTok Shop and UGC. It costs $500 a month and has about 1,000 members. He first reached Platinum, the $100,000 MRR level, from a single launch that took about a day. After attending the Platinum meetup, he ran another launch and went from about $100,000 to $450,000 MRR in a day, which one host suspects happened within an hour.

On the leaderboard he jumped from far down the rankings to about third, adding roughly $342,000 in the last 30 days. He shared a screen recording of the launch where the sale notifications came several per second, each one worth $500 a month. The hosts call it the biggest, wildest launch they have seen, and say it broke several records on Skool.

## The TikTok Live Skool Challenge

Gibran's success on TikTok ties into a wider trend. Samuel G, featured in Skool News #80, grew his $25 a month Creative Owners Club to about $200,000 a month in roughly three months using only TikTok Live, with no audience on any platform. The hosts say he is now close to $300,000 a month. He has taught the method to affiliates and other Skool creators, and it has worked for them too.

Skool is now collaborating with him on a challenge:

- **Who:** about 50 people who have a Skool community and a clear subject.
- **Commitment:** go live on TikTok for one hour every day, with no days off, from November 23 to December 22.
- **What you get:** training from Samuel G and his team, accountability, leaderboards and prizes, run inside a Skool group.
- **Cost:** free. You do not need an existing TikTok account or audience.

The hosts explain why TikTok Live suits people without a following: the algorithm listens to what you talk about and sends interested viewers into your live in real time, and you can then point them to your community. They add that at Skool Games meetups over the past two years, attendees who said they found Skool through TikTok almost always mentioned lives rather than short videos.

Applications are on the Skool apply page shown in the video. Two tips: make any video you submit publicly viewable, and check your email that week. If you have not received an invite, you were not accepted.

## Why this matters for people evaluating Skool communities

Launches like Gibran's show how fast MRR can move on Skool, which is a reason to look at growth over time rather than a single snapshot. A spike from one launch and steady month-over-month growth are different stories, and both are worth understanding before joining or comparing communities.
