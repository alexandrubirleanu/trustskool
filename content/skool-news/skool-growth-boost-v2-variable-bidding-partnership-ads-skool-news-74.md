# Skool Growth Boost V2: Deep Research, Variable Bidding and Partnership Ads

Skool News #74 reports that the rebuilt Growth Boost, now called V2, is converting attention into customers better than V1, though it has not reached V1's scale yet. The episode breaks down what changed, repeats the practical tips for qualifying, and gives a short roadmap update.

## What is different in Growth Boost V2

The hosts named four pieces of the new system.

**Deep research.** Skool built tools that study a group with a focus on members who stay past day 90, since most subscription churn happens in months one to three. The tool builds personas of those long-term members, looks at things like the most viewed videos among them, and uses that for ad targeting and messaging. The goal is not just members but day-90 retained members.

**Variable bidding.** V1 was static and assumed an average free trial conversion rate. V2 reads each group's actual trial conversion, month-one retention, predicted later retention, price, conversion rate and Growth Boost percentage, then predicts revenue over roughly 90 days to six months and sets a bid per customer. Moving the boost slider now changes the bid on Meta within about an hour. Going from 30 to 70 more than doubles what Skool can bid, which the hosts say can be the difference between no traffic and a lot.

**Partnership ads.** Instead of ads that appear to come only from Skool's profile, partnership ads show as a collaboration between the creator and Skool. In a side-by-side test using the same ads for Maker School, the partnership version performed better, so V2 is extending it to more groups.

**Creator creatives.** Skool's ads have gone from cover image carousels, to custom creatives, to clipped social content. Next, Skool plans to test letting creators upload their own ads to see whether they beat Skool's versions. Results were expected soon.

## Growth Boost tips (again)

The hosts said the worst move is to sit and wait for Growth Boost. Their checklist:

- Put your Skool link in your bio on every social platform, and check that it is actually there.
- Mention the community in your content and drive traffic to your about page.
- Improve your packaging: cover image, description and about page. Top groups in Discovery usually make their purpose clear with a crisp promise.
- Offer an annual plan at a 20 to 50% discount. Examples shown were 30% and 49%. More cash up front helps Growth Boost, and annual members naturally churn less.
- Start the slider high and lower it once boost starts working. With weak stats, Skool may already be bidding its maximum and still not winning traffic.

They also reminded owners that good things take time, and that the system is designed to find good groups eventually.

## Roadmap update

- **Reviews** are shipped, and a new default sort is coming that shows a group in its best light rather than putting the latest review first. Members can still switch to recent.
- **Languages** are live in 11 languages, including video captions and transcripts, Discovery and the UI.
- **Fast payouts** now reach about 190 countries.
- Still in progress: Growth Boost V2, an unnamed secret project, local payment methods and currencies.
