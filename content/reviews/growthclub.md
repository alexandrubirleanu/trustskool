# Growth Club Review 2026: A Free Hub for SMMA and Agency Owners

Growth Club describes itself as a free community for SMMA (social media marketing agency) owners, digital entrepreneurs and builders who are "committed to leveling up their lives and businesses." It has 4,557 members and no paid price in its record, which lines up with the word FREE that the description itself capitalizes.

## What's Actually Inside

The audience is named clearly: people who run or want to run agencies, plus a wider group of digital entrepreneurs and builders. What the description does not spell out is the content. There is no mention of a classroom, specific tactics such as cold outreach or ads, live calls, templates or a curriculum. The only stated purpose is a shared commitment to improvement, which usually points to a community-first space where peers swap questions, wins and feedback.

For a free space this size, that is a reasonable expectation: a peer network and discussion feed more than a structured program. Whether there are training modules or resources has to be checked after joining, since the listing does not promise them.

Another thing to weigh is the mix of audiences. SMMA owners, general entrepreneurs and "builders" overlap but have different needs. A person just starting an agency will ask very different questions from someone scaling a team, and a broad community can struggle to serve both well.

## Who Runs It

The owner listed is David Schlais. The data provides no biography or results for him, so the useful checks are his posts, how quickly he and moderators respond, and whether members share real outcomes.

## Pros

- Free to join as far as the record shows, so you can judge it with no financial commitment.
- A clearly named target audience in agency owners and digital entrepreneurs.
- 4,557 members offers a sizable pool of peers to ask questions and network with.
- The language is about commitment and self-improvement, which tends to attract people who want accountability.

## Cons

- No curriculum, tactics or resources are described, so the practical value is unclear until you are inside.
- "Leveling up" is a general promise with no measurable outcomes.
- A broad audience may mean advice that is generic rather than tailored to your exact stage.
- Free communities of this kind often include promotions for paid programs, which the listing neither confirms nor rules out.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Aspiring or early-stage agency owners who want a free place to meet peers, ask questions and stay motivated. It is also reasonable for people still deciding whether SMMA is a path they want to pursue.

## Who Should Skip It

Experienced agency operators who want advanced operational playbooks, or anyone who expects a structured course from a free space. If you need specific, documented training before joining, this listing does not offer enough detail to promise it.
