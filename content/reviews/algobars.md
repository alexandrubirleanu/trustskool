# AI Automated Trading Systems Review 2026: Free Community With a No-Code Trading Tool Pitch

A tool pitch sits at the center of AI Automated Trading Systems. The community, with about 3,260 members and no listed price, describes itself this way: "Build and deploy automated trading strategies in seconds. No code. Connect any broker. Claim 1,000 free credits, code START."

## What's Actually Inside

Unlike many listings, this one points to a product more than to teaching. The words about building and deploying strategies, using no code and connecting to a broker describe software functions. The mention of free credits with a promo code suggests a credit-based system, where credits are probably spent on using the tool, but the listing does not explain what a credit buys or what happens after the free ones run out.

The data does not describe lessons, trading education or live discussion. It does not name the broker integrations, the strategy types, or whether anything has been tested. "In seconds" and "any broker" are broad claims that the data cannot confirm. Trading automation also carries real financial risk, which the listing does not address at all.

## Who Runs It

The listing names Just Simple Trading as the owner of this community. That is the only information about the person included in the data behind this review, so no background, credentials or track record are described here. This appears to be a brand-style name, and the data gives no individual background.

## Pros

- Free to join the community.
- A concrete promise of no-code building, which lowers the technical barrier.
- A free credit offer lets users try the tool before paying, if that is how it works.
- About 3,260 members.

## Cons

- The listing reads like a product promotion, and the educational value is unclear.
- What credits cost after the free allowance is not stated.
- No information about risk, testing or performance of any strategy.
- The claim to connect to any broker is unverified.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

People who are curious about automating trading ideas without coding and who are willing to test a tool with small, controlled amounts of time and money after reading the terms.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone looking for trading education, risk management guidance or proven returns should skip it, since none is described. Beginners should be cautious about connecting live brokerage accounts to any automated system.
