# AI Revolution Review 2026: Free Training for Building an AI Automation Agency

AI Revolution presents itself as a "gateway to building an AI Automation Agency," and says it provides free, comprehensive training for members to build their own AIAA. It has 4,505 members and no paid price in its record. The word "comprehensive" is the owner's, and it is worth testing against what you actually find inside.

## What's Actually Inside

The promise is training on the AI automation agency model: selling automation services to businesses, usually built with AI and workflow tools. The description does not break down the training. It does not name tools, list modules, say how long the material runs, or explain how clients are found and kept, which is typically the difficult part of agency work.

Only two things are stated: the training is free, and it is meant to be comprehensive. Those two are somewhat in tension. A genuinely comprehensive agency curriculum is a lot of work to give away, so it is reasonable to ask what the free version covers and whether higher tiers or outside offers exist. The listing is silent on this.

Because AIAA is a popular and crowded niche, standing out takes more than learning the tools. The description does not address how to differentiate, price services or handle client results, so those are good questions to put to the community before relying on it.

## Who Runs It

The owner listed is Jastej Choong. The record has no biography or track record for him, so check what he posts and whether he shows verifiable client work.

## Pros

- No paid price listed, so you can look at the training for free.
- Focused on a single, specific model (AI automation agency) rather than general AI.
- 4,505 members suggests a large peer group for questions.
- Aims at a complete path from start to a running agency, according to the description.

## Cons

- "Comprehensive" is unproven, and no curriculum details are shown.
- No tools, income figures or client acquisition methods are mentioned.
- The AIAA space is crowded, and the listing does not discuss how to stand out.
- Free training may be a way into paid offers, which the data neither confirms nor excludes.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Beginners who want a free introduction to the AI automation agency idea and are willing to do the work of finding clients themselves. It suits people deciding whether to invest later in paid training.

## Who Should Skip It

Experienced agency owners, who may find a beginner pathway too basic. Also anyone who expects a ready-made client pipeline or guaranteed income, since the description promises neither.
