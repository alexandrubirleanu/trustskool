# KI Agenten Campus Review: German-Language AI Agents and Automation, Free

"KI ist nicht Trend, sondern Zukunft": AI is not a trend, it is the future. That is the line KI Agenten Campus puts forward, and it frames the whole offer. The community is written in German, has 2,719 members and shows no price, so we treat it as free.

## What's Actually Inside

The description says this is a community for everyone who wants to master AI agents and automation. Two topics are named: agents, which are AI systems that carry out tasks on their own, and automation, which usually means connecting tools so repetitive work runs without manual effort. Beyond that, the listing does not break down modules, tools, templates or call schedules.

So the honest summary is a topic and a language rather than a syllabus. Member counts were stable at 2,704 on one date and 2,719 on the next, then flat, so growth looks modest rather than explosive in the days we track.

For German speakers, a dedicated space matters. Most agent and automation tutorials are in English, and a local community can make it easier to ask questions in your own words.

## Who Runs It

Niklas Hansen is the listed owner. The data does not tell us his background, and we will not guess at one. The platform also shows an affiliate rate of 0 percent, which means referral commissions are not offered to members.

## Pros

- No price listed, so access appears free.
- Narrow, current topic: AI agents and automation.
- German-language, which lowers the barrier for local learners.
- 2,719 members gives a decent pool for questions and examples.

## Cons

- No detail on lessons, tools or live support.
- Free communities sometimes funnel members toward paid products, and the listing does not say.
- Agent tooling changes fast, so tutorials can age within months.
- No affiliate programme if you hoped to recommend it for commission.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

German-speaking freelancers, small business owners and curious technical beginners who want to see how AI agents are built and used, and who prefer learning in their own language at no cost.

## Who Should Skip It

Developers who already build agents professionally will probably find the material too introductory. Anyone who needs a guaranteed method for earning money should look elsewhere, since the listing promises none. If you do not read German, the community will be hard to use at all.
