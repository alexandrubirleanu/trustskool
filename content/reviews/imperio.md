# Imperio Agéntico Review 2026: A Spanish-Language AI Agents Community at $59/Month

Imperio Agéntico is a Spanish-language Skool community with 3,015 members and a price of $59 per month. Its description, translated, says: "Here we save time, get to the point, and grow seriously with agents, systems, vibe coding, and AI automations."

## What's Actually Inside

The original text reads "Aquí ahorramos tiempo, vamos al grano y crecemos en serio con agentes, sistemas, vibe coding y automatizaciones con IA." Two things stand out.

First, the tone. "Ahorramos tiempo" and "vamos al grano" promise efficiency and no filler, which is a positioning statement about style. Second, the topics: agents, systems, vibe coding, and AI automations. Vibe coding generally refers to building software by describing what you want to an AI assistant instead of writing every line by hand. Agents and automations point to workflows where AI performs multi-step tasks.

What the description does not tell us is how any of this is taught. There is no mention of course modules, live calls, templates, or support. It does not say which tools are covered, what level of technical skill is expected, or whether the goal is to build for your own business, for clients, or both. "Crecemos en serio" (we grow seriously) implies business growth, but no outcomes are defined.

Because the community operates in Spanish, it is aimed at Spanish speakers. That is a real advantage for the audience, as much AI education is only available in English. Whether the tools and examples inside are localized is something to check.

At $59 per month, the cost is $708 over a year. AI tooling changes quickly, so check whether the content is actively updated before committing long term.
