# Publisher Mastery (KDP Course) Review 2026: Free Community on Selling Books Online

Selling books is the whole proposition of Publisher Mastery (KDP Course), a free Skool community with about 3,216 members. The description is brief: "Learn how to make passive income online for free selling books." The KDP in the name refers to Amazon's Kindle Direct Publishing, a self-publishing service.

## What's Actually Inside

The listing says members will learn to make passive income by selling books, and that this is free. It does not say what type of books (low-content, fiction, nonfiction), how they are produced, how they are marketed, or what tools are used. It also does not describe the format of the "course" mentioned in the name.

The phrase passive income deserves some care. Publishing books can generate ongoing sales, but the work of creating and promoting them is not passive at the start, and the data includes no earnings figures. The word free appears in the description, yet many communities of this type also promote paid upgrades, which the listing neither mentions nor rules out. As a rule, newcomers to self-publishing should expect to spend time on research, writing or sourcing content, formatting and promotion, and should check early on whether the community explains each of those steps or only the sales side.

## Who Runs It

The listing names Ken Fornari as the owner of this community. That is the only information about the person included in the data behind this review, so no background, credentials or track record are described here.

## Pros

- Free to join.
- A well-defined side-income topic with a known platform (KDP).
- Beginner-friendly in tone, with a simple promise.
- About 3,216 members.

## Cons

- No explanation of the book types or publishing methods taught.
- No details on course format or length.
- "Passive income" is an optimistic label, and no results are given.
- The listing does not say whether any paid tier exists.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Beginners curious about self-publishing who want a free introduction to the KDP route and are ready to verify claims with their own small tests.

## Who Should Skip It

Writers wanting editorial or creative-writing coaching should look elsewhere, as the listing focuses on income. Anyone expecting reliable passive earnings should skip it, since none are documented.
