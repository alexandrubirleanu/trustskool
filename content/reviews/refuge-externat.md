# Le Refuge des Externes Review 2026: Is This Free French Medical Study Community Worth Joining?

With 7,338 members, Le refuge des externes ☕️ is one of the larger French-language Skool communities aimed at "externes," the French medical students working through their clinical externship years ahead of the EDN, France's national licensing exam that determines residency placement. The community's own description promises kindness ("bienveillance") and motivation to help members get through that exam.

## What's Actually Inside

The description is short and doesn't detail a curriculum, question bank, or study schedule. It frames the space primarily as emotional and motivational support for a famously stressful stretch of French medical training, rather than as a structured revision course. That's a meaningfully different offer than a paid EDN question-bank subscription or a formal tutoring program, so students should go in expecting peer camaraderie and encouragement more than graded practice material.

## Who Runs It

Robin Rigaudeau is listed as the community's owner on Skool. No further background is available in the data reviewed here, so this review doesn't speculate about medical credentials, teaching experience, or how any exam guidance inside the group is vetted.

## Pros

- Free to join, with no cost barrier for externs already facing years of expensive medical training.
- A large, active-looking membership base (over 7,300) for a fairly specific niche, French medical students preparing for the EDN.
- Explicitly framed around emotional support and motivation, which can matter during a demanding, high-stakes exam cycle.

## Cons

- The description gives no detail on study structure, content quality, or whether any clinical or exam material is reviewed by qualified educators.
- No independent reviews of this specific community were found, so students can't verify how active or useful the day-to-day discussion actually is before joining.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

French medical externs preparing for the EDN who want a free, peer-support space for motivation and encouragement alongside their existing revision materials, rather than a replacement for one. Given how isolating the externship years can feel, students who simply want to know others are going through the same grind may get real value here, even without a formal syllabus attached.

## Who Should Skip It

Students looking for a structured question bank, graded practice exams, or expert-led EDN tutoring should look for a dedicated exam-prep resource instead, since this community's own description doesn't claim to offer that.
