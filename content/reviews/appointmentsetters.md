## Cons

- No detail on how job access works or how many members get placed.
- The phrase "expert guidance" is not explained.
- High ticket sales roles are often commission-based, and the description says nothing about pay structures or what a realistic first role looks like.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

People seriously considering a move into remote sales who want a low-cost way to learn the basics of setting and closing while getting a look at available roles. It suits those who are prepared to put in practice time and treat any job access as a bonus rather than a guarantee.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone expecting guaranteed employment, a salary, or quick income. If you are uncomfortable with commission-heavy work or phone-based selling, the roles this community points toward may not suit you, no matter how good the training is. Experienced closers with an existing pipeline of offers likely have less to gain.
