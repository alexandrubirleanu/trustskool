# AI Launch Review 2026: Free AI Business Ideas Community

AI Launch, branded on Skool as "AI MAKE MONEY," is a free community pitched around learning AI and turning it into income, built by Jens Dietrich. It has grown to 6,496 members on a promise of practical AI business ideas rather than abstract AI theory.

## What's Actually Inside

The description leads with two direct promises: learn AI, and make money with AI. It backs that up with a specific-sounding number, 555 validated business ideas that supposedly require $0 to start, plus the goal of building AI products that generate income. That's a lot packed into a short pitch, and "555 validated" is the kind of precise-sounding figure that's worth treating skeptically until you can see the actual list inside the community, since the listing gives no source or validation method for that number.

"Build AI products that make money" suggests some members are meant to move beyond consuming ideas into shipping their own tools, though the description doesn't say what kind of support, templates, or technical guidance is provided to get there.

## Pros

- Free to join, so testing the offer costs nothing upfront.
- A large stated volume of business ideas (555) could genuinely help with the common "I don't know what to build" bottleneck, if the list holds up.
- Framing around AI products, not just AI tips, suggests some members are pushed toward building rather than passively consuming content.

## Cons

- "555 validated business ideas" is an unverified, precise-sounding claim with no stated validation method or source in the listing.
- No detail on what "make AI products" actually involves technically, whether that's no-code tools, coding, or reselling other people's AI wrappers.
- We found no independent reviews of this community beyond its own Skool listing.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Beginners exploring AI-based income ideas who want a free starting point and a large volume of ideas to sift through themselves.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who wants pre-vetted, rigorously validated business models rather than a large volume of unverified ideas should treat the "555 validated" claim with caution and dig into specifics before investing real time.

Since joining costs nothing, a reasonable approach is to browse a sample of the "555 ideas" directly inside before assuming the number reflects genuinely tested, working businesses rather than a large brainstormed list.
