# AI Make Money Review 2026: Free Skool Community for AI Business Ideas

Stylized branding aside, "AI Make Money" states its pitch in four short lines: learn AI, make money with AI, get access to 555 validated business ideas that cost $0 to start, and build AI products that generate income. It's a bold, numbers-driven promise, and it's attracted real traction, 6,496 members as of the latest tracked date, with fast growth in the most recent weeks (up from 6,288 members in the space of a few days).

## What's Actually Inside

The headline offer here is specific in a way many "make money with AI" communities aren't: a stated library of 555 "validated business ideas" that require $0 to start. That's a concrete asset, assuming it holds up to scrutiny once inside, and it suggests the community is organized at least partly around an idea database rather than pure discussion. The description also points to "building AI products that make money," which implies some members go beyond simply picking an idea from the list and move into actually shipping tools or products. What the description doesn't confirm is how those 555 ideas are validated, what "validated" means in this context, or whether there's structured guidance for turning an idea into an actual product versus just a list to browse.

## Who Runs It

Jens Dietrich is listed as the community's owner in the platform data. He joined the Skool platform in June 2025 and shows strong recent engagement: 21 active days in the last 30 and 81 in the last 90, alongside 1,266 total contributions and 6,605 followers. The data also lists an affiliate percentage of 40, indicating the community participates in Skool's affiliate program at that rate. Together, this points to an actively managed, platform-savvy operation rather than an abandoned group.

## Pros

- Free to join, with the core idea list also framed as $0-to-start.
- A specific, countable asset (555 business ideas) rather than a vague promise of "AI training."
- Fast recent membership growth, suggesting strong current interest and visibility.
- An actively engaged owner, with activity on 21 of the last 30 days.

## Cons

- "Validated" is not defined in the description, so it's unclear what vetting, if any, those 555 ideas went through.
- No detail is given on how members move from an idea on the list to an actual working AI product.
- The community's participation in Skool's affiliate program (a 40 percent rate) means some of its growth may be driven by affiliate promotion rather than organic word of mouth, which is worth keeping in mind when evaluating claims.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Beginners curious about AI-driven side income who want a free, idea-generation starting point rather than a deep technical course.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who has already validated their own AI business idea and needs execution-focused, advanced support, since this community's core hook is the idea list itself rather than confirmed advanced implementation guidance.
