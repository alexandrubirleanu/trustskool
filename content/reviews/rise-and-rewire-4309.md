# Rise and Rewire Review 2026: Can This Free Community Land You Multiple Interviews in 30 Days?

"Get multiple interviews in 30 days" is a bold, specific claim, and it's the headline promise behind Rise and Rewire, a free Skool community that has grown to 5,182 members around job search and career change strategy.

## What's Actually Inside

The description is built around motivational, career-pivot language, "refuse to stay stuck," "rise to your potential," "rewire your career," paired with one concrete, testable claim: multiple interviews within 30 days using "strategies that actually work." What those strategies actually are, resume rewriting, LinkedIn optimization, networking tactics, or something else, isn't specified. There's also no detail on what counts as a successful outcome for members who don't land interviews in that window, or how the 30-day claim was measured, if it was measured at all. Job search advice communities often blend genuinely useful tactics, like tailoring applications and following up with hiring managers, with generic motivational content, and without more specifics it's hard to tell from the listing which side of that mix this one leans toward.

## Who Runs It

Marcel Lombard is listed as the owner of this community on Skool. No further independent background or credentials are available in the data reviewed here.

## Pros

- Free to join, so job seekers can test the strategies without financial risk during what's often an already stressful, income-constrained period.
- A specific, measurable claim, multiple interviews in 30 days, gives a concrete benchmark to hold the community accountable to, at least in theory.
- A member base over 5,100 suggests the pitch resonates with a meaningful number of active job seekers.

## Cons

- The "multiple interviews in 30 days" claim isn't backed by cited data, case studies, or a stated methodology in the description.
- No independent reviews of this community were found beyond its own Skool listing, so outcomes can't be verified from outside sources.
- The description doesn't specify which industries or career levels the strategies are aimed at, so fit may vary widely by field.
- Job markets and hiring practices vary enormously by industry and region, and a single 30-day framework is unlikely to apply equally everywhere.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Job seekers currently in an active search who want structured strategy and community accountability, and who are willing to test the 30-day claim themselves rather than treating it as guaranteed.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone expecting a guaranteed outcome, rather than a set of strategies to try, should adjust expectations before joining, and job seekers in highly specialized or niche fields may want to confirm the strategies apply to their industry specifically.
