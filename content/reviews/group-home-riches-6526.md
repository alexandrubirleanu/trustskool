## What's Actually Inside

The full description is one line: "Mastermind for Group Home Entrepreneurs and Investors."

That is brief, but it does tell you three useful things. First, the niche is specific: group homes, not real estate investing in general. Second, the audience includes two overlapping groups, operators who run group homes as a business and investors who may own the property or fund the venture. Third, it calls itself a mastermind, which usually implies peer discussion and shared problem solving rather than a self-paced course library.

Beyond that, the listing is silent. And if you want a structured course with a defined curriculum, the listing gives no sign that this is one.
