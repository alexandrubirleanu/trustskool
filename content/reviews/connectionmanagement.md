# Connection Community Review 2026: Free Skool Group for Danish Leaders

Leadership development communities are common on Skool, but most are built for a global, English-speaking audience. Connection Community takes a different angle, pitching itself, per its own description, as "Denmark's best community for leaders," a space for anyone interested in leadership who wants to develop their skills alongside other leaders. That's a clear, if bold, positioning statement, and the community has built a steady base of 6,489 members around it.

## What's Actually Inside

Translating the Danish description, the core promise is peer-based leadership development: a place for people already in, or interested in, leadership roles to grow their competencies "together with other leaders." That phrasing leans toward a collaborative, discussion-oriented format rather than a one-directional course where a single instructor delivers content downward. The description doesn't specify whether the community includes structured programming, live sessions, or resources beyond ongoing discussion, so the exact mix of peer exchange versus formal training content isn't confirmed by the data here. It's categorized under Skool's self-improvement heading, which fits a leadership-skills focus broadly, though it doesn't narrow down whether the emphasis is on people management, strategic leadership, or something more personal-development-oriented.

## Cons

- The description doesn't detail specific programming, courses, or session formats beyond general peer development.
- No information is available on the owner's background or day-to-day activity level in the group.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Danish-speaking leaders and managers looking for a free, peer-oriented space to discuss leadership challenges with others in similar roles.

## Who Should Skip It
