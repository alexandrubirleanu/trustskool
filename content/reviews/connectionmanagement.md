# Connection Community Review 2026: Danish Leadership Development Group

Connection Community bills itself as Denmark's best community for leaders, a gathering point for anyone interested in leadership who wants to develop their skills alongside other leaders. It's free to join, run by Martin Mouritzen, and has reached 6,489 members.

## What's Actually Inside

The description centers on leadership as a discipline: developing competencies as a leader in the company of peers doing the same. That's a concrete professional niche rather than generic motivational content, though the listing stops short of describing format, whether it's peer discussion, structured courses, live sessions, or a mix. It also doesn't specify what kind of leaders it's for, first-time managers, senior executives, or a mix of experience levels, and the "Denmark's best" claim is a self-description rather than something independently verified.

Because the group is Danish-language and Denmark-focused, non-Danish speakers or people outside that professional context will likely find less direct relevance, even though leadership principles themselves are widely transferable. Nothing in the listing names a specific leadership framework, so it's not clear whether the community follows one methodology or draws on a mix of member experience and general management practice.

A group built around peer exchange between leaders, rather than a single instructor lecturing at members, tends to work best when enough active, engaged participants are actually posting and discussing, so it's worth checking the level of activity in the community's posts before deciding it's a fit.

## Pros

- Free to join, removing cost as a barrier to testing the community.
- A clear professional focus, leadership development, rather than vague self-improvement framing.
- Peer-based structure, learning alongside other leaders suggests active peer exchange, not just one-way content delivery.

## Cons

- No detail on format, session frequency, or which leadership experience levels the community is actually built for.
- "Denmark's best" is a self-made claim with no independent verification behind it in the listing.
- We found no independent reviews of this community beyond its own Skool listing.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Danish-speaking leaders and managers who want a peer community focused specifically on leadership skills, at no cost, and who are comfortable investigating format and fit directly.

## Who Should Skip It

Non-Danish speakers will hit an immediate language barrier, and anyone looking for leadership content built for a global or English-speaking audience will likely find better-matched alternatives elsewhere.
