# Connection Community Review 2026: Free Danish-Language Skool Community for Leaders

Connection Community has grown to nearly 6,500 members with a pitch aimed squarely at leaders: described as "Denmark's best community for leaders," a place for anyone interested in leadership who wants to develop their skills alongside other leaders.

## What's Actually Inside

The description keeps its focus tight on one professional role, leadership, rather than business ownership or entrepreneurship broadly. That distinction matters: this reads as a community for people already in, or moving into, leadership positions within organizations, not founders building their own company from scratch. It's categorized under self-improvement on Skool, which fits a peer-development focus over hard business tactics. The listing frames the value as developing competencies "together with other leaders," suggesting a peer-learning or discussion-based format rather than a formal course, though the exact mix of live events, discussion, and resources isn't specified.

Recent tracking shows membership holding essentially flat around 6,488 to 6,489, indicating an established, steady community rather than one riding a recent surge of new sign-ups. For a leadership-focused group, that kind of stability could be a positive sign, since consistent, familiar faces tend to matter more for peer development than sheer volume of new members joining each week.

## Who Runs It

Martin Mouritzen is listed as the community's owner. No further background beyond that listing is available from the data reviewed here, so any claims about leadership expertise or credentials would need to be verified elsewhere.

## Pros

- Free to join, so there's no cost barrier to seeing whether the peer discussion matches a member's needs.
- Specific role focus on leadership, not general business or self-improvement, which should keep conversations relevant to people managing others.
- A solidly sized membership near 6,500, offering a reasonable pool of peers for discussion and networking.

## Cons

- The description doesn't detail format, so members expecting structured coursework versus open peer discussion should confirm before joining.
- "Best community for leaders" is a marketing claim not independently verified here.
- This community is tagged Danish-language, so non-Danish speakers won't be able to participate in discussions or access content.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Danish-speaking leaders and managers who want a free, peer-based community focused specifically on leadership development.

## Who Should Skip It

Non-Danish speakers, and anyone looking for a formal, structured leadership course rather than an open peer community.
