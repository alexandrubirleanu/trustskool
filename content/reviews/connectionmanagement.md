# Connection Community Review 2026: Denmark's Free Leadership Community, Worth Joining?

Connection Community makes a bold claim right in its own description: "Danmarks bedste community for ledere," Denmark's best community for leaders. Bold claim aside, what it actually is, is a free, Danish-language Skool community with 6,489 members built for people interested in leadership and developing their skills alongside other managers.

## What's Actually Inside

The description frames this as a peer space: a place for anyone interested in leadership who wants to develop their skills together with other leaders. That's a peer-exchange model rather than a stated formal curriculum, and the listing doesn't detail workshops, courses, live calls, or any specific content format. It also doesn't say whether the leadership focus skews toward a particular industry or company size, or whether it's aimed at new managers versus more senior executives, so prospective members are largely left to find that out by joining. The "Denmark's best" line is self-proclaimed marketing language, not an independently verified ranking, so it's worth reading as positioning rather than fact.

## Who Runs It

Martin Mouritzen is listed as the community's owner. No further background, certifications, or leadership credentials are given beyond that name, so this review sticks to that basic attribution rather than assuming a particular professional history.

## Pros

- Free to join, with no financial barrier to trying it.
- A large membership for a fairly specific niche, 6,489 members interested in leadership development.
- Clearly focused on managers and leaders rather than generic self-improvement.
- Being a peer-driven community, it may surface real, current challenges other Danish managers are facing, rather than pre-packaged theory.

## Cons

- "Denmark's best" is an unverified, self-applied claim with no independent metric behind it.
- No detail on format, session cadence, or what specific resources members get access to.
- Danish-language only, limiting it to Danish speakers.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Danish-speaking managers and leaders who want a free space to connect with peers and work on leadership skills together, particularly those who value learning from other practitioners over a formal top-down curriculum.

## Who Should Skip It

Non-Danish speakers will hit an obvious language barrier. Anyone looking for a formally accredited leadership certification, rather than an informal peer community, should also look elsewhere, since none is described here. People who prefer one-on-one executive coaching over group peer exchange may also find the format less suited to their needs.
