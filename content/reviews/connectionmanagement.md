# Connection Community Review 2026: A Danish Leadership Development Group

Connection Community calls itself, in its own words, Denmark's best community for leaders, a place for anyone interested in leadership who wants to develop their skills together with other leaders. It's a Danish-language Skool group in the self-improvement category, free to join, and has grown to 6,489 members.

## What's Actually Inside

Unlike a lot of self-improvement communities that stay vague about audience, this one is specific on that front: it's built for people who already identify as leaders or managers, not for a general personal-development audience. The phrase "sammen med andre ledere" (together with other leaders) points to a peer-development model, leaders learning from and alongside other leaders, rather than a top-down course delivered by a single instructor. That's a meaningful structural signal even though the description doesn't detail specific leadership frameworks, industries represented, or whether the format leans toward live discussion, written content, or both.

The "Danmarks bedste" (Denmark's best) claim is standard marketing language and isn't something a directory listing can verify independently, but the specificity of "for leaders" as the target audience is a real, checkable detail rather than hype. Member growth stayed essentially flat across the tracked dates, moving from 6,488 up to 6,489, which suggests a stable, established group rather than one in a rapid growth phase right now.

## Who Runs It

The community's owner is listed as Martin Mouritzen. Beyond that name, no further public biographical detail is available from the data reviewed here.

## Pros

- Free to join, with no cost barrier to connecting with the leadership-focused peer group.
- A membership base above 6,400 suggests solid reach for a leadership community built in a smaller-language market.
- A clearly defined audience, working leaders and managers, rather than a general self-improvement pitch.

## Cons

- The description doesn't name specific leadership frameworks, formats, or facilitator credentials, so the exact content mix isn't confirmed by the listing alone.
- Everything about the group is in Danish, and no independent reviews of Connection Community beyond its own Skool listing were found during this review.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Danish-speaking leaders and managers looking for a free, peer-driven community to develop leadership skills alongside others in similar roles.

## Who Should Skip It

Non-Danish speakers, and anyone outside a leadership or management role looking for general personal-development content instead.
