## Pros

- At $5 a month, the cost of testing it for a few weeks is minimal.
- A clear, finish-line goal (a publish-ready story) instead of a vague promise to "improve your art."
- The pitch bundles instruction with coaching and peers, which can help with the accountability that solo creators often lack.
- A membership of 2,451 is large enough that you're likely to find people working on similar projects.

## Cons

- The format and frequency of the coaching aren't specified, so "real coaching" is hard to evaluate before joining.
- "College-level teaching" is an unverified claim from the description.
- No information on whether the focus leans toward writing, art, lettering, or publishing logistics.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Aspiring comic creators, whether writers, artists, or both, who have an idea in progress and want structure plus a group of people who understand the medium. The low monthly price also suits hobbyists who aren't ready to spend on a full course.

## Who Should Skip It

Experienced professionals who already have publishing workflows and an editor or peer network may not need it. Anyone who specifically wants intensive one-on-one mentorship should confirm what the coaching actually looks like before assuming it's included at this price point.
