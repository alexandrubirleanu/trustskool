# 10K TECH Review: A Spanish-Language Community for Automotive Technicians

Most Skool communities chase online business, which makes 10K TECH refreshingly different. It is a Spanish-language community with 2,704 members for automotive technicians, and it says its purpose is to keep members "up to date" and help them learn the right skills to become a "10K technician."

## What's Actually Inside

The description gives us the trade (automotive technical work), the language (Spanish) and the goal (staying current and building skills). It does not explain what "10K" means, whether it points to income, a skill level or a brand, so we leave that interpretation alone.

No price is listed, which we read as free access. Course lists, diagnostic tools, live sessions and brand coverage are not described.

For a trade community, the useful test is practicality. Look for real diagnostic walkthroughs, discussion of modern vehicle systems, and members who answer troubleshooting questions with detail rather than slogans. The more the posts resemble shop-floor problems, the better the fit.

Trade communities live or die by how useful the answers are. A good test is to search for a fault you have seen recently and read how members handled it. Do they ask for scan data, discuss measurements and explain the logic, or do they just guess? Another sign is whether newer vehicle topics, such as hybrids, electronic systems and software updates, appear alongside classic mechanical work. With 2,704 members, there should be enough varied experience to produce good threads if the culture supports it.

## Cons

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone outside the automotive trade, or technicians who need accredited certification or hands-on workshop training that a community cannot replace.
