# High-Ticket Sales Society Review 2026: Should You Trust the $10k Per Month Closer Pitch?

Remote closing has become one of the most heavily marketed career paths in the online business world, and the income figures attached to it tend to be large. High-Ticket Sales Society leans into that. Its description invites people to "learn, share, and thrive as a remote closer," and it names a specific range: earning $10k to $25k or more per month.

Those numbers are the community's own claim. Nothing in the data verifies them, and they should be read as a pitch rather than a typical outcome. What can be confirmed is that the group is free and lists 2,419 members.

## What's Actually Inside

Based on the description, the community is about remote high-ticket sales, meaning closing expensive offers over the phone or video for other businesses, usually on commission. The wording "learn, share, and thrive" points to a mix of education and peer discussion.

That is about as far as the listing goes. There is no mention of a course, scripts, role-play sessions, job boards, or introductions to companies hiring closers. Any of those could exist, but none are described in the data, so this review cannot say they are there.

## Who Runs It

Chris Campbell is listed as the owner. No further background is available in the data reviewed here, and this page does not speculate about his sales record or whether he earns in the range the description mentions.

## Pros

- Free to join, which removes the main risk for someone just exploring sales as a career.
- A focused topic: remote closing specifically, not general entrepreneurship.
- Peer sharing is part of the stated purpose, which can be useful for practicing objection handling and comparing offers.

## Cons

- The $10k to $25k+ per month figure is an unverified marketing claim, and income in commission sales varies widely.
- No details on training format, materials, or job placement support.
- High-ticket closing communities are often a front door to paid programs; the listing does not say whether that applies here.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

People seriously considering remote sales who want a free place to learn the vocabulary, ask questions, and hear from others doing the work. Existing closers looking for a peer network might also find value in the sharing side.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone expecting a guaranteed income or a direct path to a closing job should be cautious, since nothing here supports either. If you dislike sales calls, commission-based pay, or income-focused marketing, this is probably not your space.
