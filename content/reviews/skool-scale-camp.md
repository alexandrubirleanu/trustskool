Midlife women entrepreneurs looking to grow on Skool have a free option in Skool Scale Camp, a 5,398-member community built around what its own description calls a "Community to Cashflow Bundle."

## What's Actually Inside

The description frames the audience narrowly: midlife women entrepreneurs who are "ready to grow on Skool, get visible, and create consistent MRR" (monthly recurring revenue). It also references a specific free bundle running July 27 to 31, 2026, which suggests the community may be tied to a limited-time event or launch window rather than functioning purely as an evergreen resource. Beyond the visibility and MRR framing, the description doesn't spell out whether the content is live coaching, templates, courses, or a mix, so prospective members are working from a fairly high-level pitch.

The "Community to Cashflow Bundle" naming is worth pausing on, since it implies the free offer is a packaged set of resources rather than a single lesson or workshop. Bundles like this are common in the Skool ecosystem as a low-friction entry point into a broader paid ecosystem, so it's reasonable to expect that some portion of what's inside points toward a follow-on paid program, even though nothing in the listing itself confirms an upsell. The niche focus on age and life stage, midlife specifically, also sets this apart from the many "women in business" communities that don't specify an age range at all, which could matter for members who want peers navigating similar career-stage or life-stage challenges.

## Who Runs It

Brenda Rigney is listed as the owner of this community on Skool. No further independent background or credentials are available in the data reviewed here.

## Pros

- Free to join, with no financial barrier to testing the content.
- A clear target audience (midlife women entrepreneurs) rather than a generic "everyone welcome" pitch.
- Specific goals named in the description, visibility and consistent MRR, give a concrete sense of what success is meant to look like.

## Cons

- The description ties the community to a specific five-day free bundle window, and it's unclear from the data whether the broader community stays active outside that event period.
- No details are given on format, live calls, courses, or templates, so members won't know what to expect until after joining.
- No independent reviews of this community were found beyond its own Skool listing.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Midlife women entrepreneurs already building or growing a business on Skool who want a free, community-specific resource focused on visibility and recurring revenue, and who don't mind that the offer is tied to a bundle rather than an open-ended curriculum.

## Who Should Skip It

Entrepreneurs outside the stated demographic, or those looking for a fully built-out course library rather than a bundle-style or event-driven community, may want to look elsewhere first.
