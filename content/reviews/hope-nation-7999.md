# Hope Nation Review: A Free Bible Study Group on Skool

Hope Nation bills itself as "the #1 Bible Study community on Skool," and its goal is simple: help members read and understand the Bible. With 2,677 members and no price on the listing, it is a free English-language space for faith-based learning.

## What's Actually Inside

The description is short on structure but clear on purpose. Members come to learn how to read scripture and grasp what it says. It does not name a reading plan, a denomination, live study times or a classroom, so we cannot describe those.

The "#1" claim is the community's own wording. We have no ranking data in the description to confirm it, and a Skool community this size should be judged on activity and tone, not on a title.

If you join, look at how discussions are moderated. A good study group welcomes beginners, keeps questions respectful and explains context rather than just quoting verses.

If you are deciding whether to stay, watch how newcomers are treated. A study group should make it easy to ask basic questions without embarrassment, and leaders should point to the text rather than argue. Also look at how often content is posted and whether it follows a pattern, such as daily readings or weekly discussions. Consistency matters more than size for a habit like Bible reading, and a community of 2,677 can sustain regular rhythms if the owner commits to one.
