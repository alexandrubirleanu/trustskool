# Akademia 100k Review 2026: A Polish Path to Scaling a Personal Brand?

Most creator business communities on Skool are in English and price their goals in dollars. Akademia 100k is different: it is written in Polish and frames its targets in złoty. With 2,146 members and no price shown in the listing, it is one of the larger free spaces for Polish-speaking creators in the directory.

## What's Actually Inside

The description, translated, presents it as a community and courses for creators who want to build a personal brand and scale their business to 10, 50 or even 100 thousand złoty per month.

So the listing points to two components:

1. **Courses**, plural, though their titles, length and format are not specified.
2. **A community** of creators working on similar goals.

The main themes are personal branding and business scaling. The description does not say which channels are emphasized (Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, newsletters), what kind of products members are expected to sell, or whether there are live sessions or coaching.

The revenue tiers in the description, 10k, 50k and 100k zł per month, are aspirational targets used as framing. They are not presented as results the community has delivered, and nothing in the data verifies how many members, if any, reach them. The "100k" in the name refers to the top tier of that range.

## Who Runs It

Rafał Sienkiewicz is listed as the owner. No further background is available in the data, so this review makes no assumptions about his own business, audience size, or track record. If you are deciding whether to follow someone's advice on scaling to six figures in złoty, it is reasonable to look for evidence of their own experience before investing significant time.

A 10% affiliate commission is listed for the community.

## Pros

- No price listed, which makes it easy to try.
- Content in Polish, built around the Polish market and currency.
- Combines courses with a peer community rather than offering only one or the other.
- A clear focus on personal brand as the engine for business growth.

## Cons

- The course contents are not described at all.
- Income tiers in the description are goals, not verified outcomes.
- No category is assigned in the listing, and the description is broad enough to cover many kinds of creators.
- No information on how active the community is or how support works.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Polish-speaking creators, freelancers and solo entrepreneurs who already have some audience or offer and want structured help turning a personal brand into a more predictable income. It may also suit people early in the journey who want free course material in their own language and a peer group thinking about the same problems.

## Who Should Skip It

If you are looking for help with a product business, an agency with staff, or anything not centered on your own personal brand, the stated focus probably will not match. People who do not read Polish will not be able to use the content. And anyone expecting a defined system with promised results should note that the listing offers goals, not guarantees.
