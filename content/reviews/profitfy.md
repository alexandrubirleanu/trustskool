# PROFITFY Review 2026: Is Copy-and-Paste Trading Worth $97 a Month?

PROFITFY is a Spanish-language trading community on Skool with 2,389 members and a listed price of $97 USD per month. That makes it one of the paid options in this part of the directory, and the price deserves to be weighed against what the community actually claims to offer.

## What's Actually Inside

Translated from Spanish, the description calls PROFITFY the number one trading community "in results" and says members can earn consistently just by copying and pasting, with no complications and no risk.

Taken at face value, that points to a copy trading model, where members replicate trades or signals shared inside the group instead of developing their own analysis. The description does not name the markets traded (forex, crypto, stocks, or futures), the platforms used, how signals are delivered, or whether there is any educational content alongside them.

The "no risk" line needs to be addressed directly. All trading carries risk, and copying someone else's trades does not remove it. The claim of being number one in results is also the community's own statement, and nothing in the data reviewed here verifies performance figures, track records, or member outcomes. Anyone considering a subscription should treat these as marketing claims and ask for verifiable records before paying.

## Who Runs It

Mauricio Ceballos Jimenez is listed as the owner. No further background is available in the data, so this review does not comment on his trading history or qualifications.

## Pros

- A clear, simple model for people who want a hands-off approach rather than learning technical analysis.
- Monthly billing at $97, so members are not locked into a large one-time payment and can cancel if results disappoint.
- Spanish-language content for traders who prefer to learn and communicate in Spanish.

## Cons

- The "no risk" promise is not realistic for any form of trading and is a red flag in itself.
- Performance claims ("number one in results," "consistent gains") are unverified.
- No information on markets, methods, or risk management.
- At $97 a month, the subscription is a recurring cost that has to be covered by trading gains before members come out ahead.
- Copying trades without understanding them can leave members unable to judge when a strategy stops working.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Spanish-speaking traders who already understand market risk, have capital they can afford to lose, and want to evaluate a copy trading service on a month-to-month basis. It may also suit experienced traders curious to compare someone else's calls against their own.

## Who Should Skip It

Beginners who are drawn in by the words "no risk." Anyone who would be trading with money they need for bills or savings. And anyone who wants to actually learn to trade, since nothing in the description suggests education is the focus.
