## Cons

There's also no detail on Lauri's teaching background or credentials beyond the method being named after them. We found no independent reviews of Spanish After Hours beyond its own Skool listing.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

People who've struggled to stick with traditional Spanish classes or apps and want to try a differently-framed, community-based approach for free before committing elsewhere.

## Who Should Skip It

Learners who need a clearly defined curriculum, proficiency track, or certification path from the outset may find the "not boring" framing too light on specifics to commit to right away. Anyone who has already reached an advanced conversational level may also outgrow a community pitched broadly at making learning fun rather than at a specific proficiency tier.
