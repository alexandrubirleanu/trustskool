# Online Coaches And Experts Review 2026: A Free Group for Coaches and Course Creators

Coaches and course creators rarely lack expertise. What they often lack is an audience that turns into paying clients. Online Coaches And Experts addresses that gap directly and is free: a Skool group with 3,006 members.

The description calls it "a free group for online coaches and course creators on all things: driving more traffic, generating leads and converting more eyeballs into clients."

## What's Actually Inside

The sentence maps to three stages of a basic funnel. Traffic is getting attention, leads are capturing contact details or interest, and conversion is turning that interest into clients. The phrase "all things" is broad, though, and it is the part to be careful with. It does not say which platforms are covered, whether advertising or organic content is the focus, or whether the lessons are structured.

Notably, "a free group" is how it describes itself, which sets expectations: this reads like a discussion and resource space more than a premium course. The same description is also a typical top-of-funnel pitch, and some free groups exist to introduce the owner's paid services. Nothing in the data confirms that here, but it is a fair question to ask on the about page.

For its niche, 3,006 members makes it mid-sized, with plenty of peers in the same position, which can be useful for feedback and swapping ideas.

## Who Runs It

Jon Penberthy is listed as the owner on Skool. No further verified background, client results or credentials are available in the data reviewed, so none are included.

## Pros

- Free to join, with no payment barrier.
- A clearly defined audience: online coaches and course creators.
- Covers the full path from attention to clients, rather than a single tactic.
- Peers in the same business model can offer practical, relatable feedback.

## Cons

- "All things" gives little concrete detail on content or format.
- No named platforms or methods, so it is difficult to judge relevance beforehand.
- Marketing advice is highly context dependent, and what worked for one coach may not work for another. Nothing here should be read as a promise of leads or income.
- Free groups can be uneven in moderation and quality.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Coaches, consultants and course creators who are early in building an audience and want a free place to exchange ideas about getting traffic and leads. It also suits people who like testing a community before investing in a paid program.

## Who Should Skip It

Established creators with a working acquisition system may find the topics too general. Anyone expecting a detailed step-by-step curriculum, one-to-one coaching or guaranteed outcomes should look at paid programs with transparent syllabi. If you are not selling coaching or courses, the material is probably not designed for you. Before joining, check how active the feed is, whether posts come from members or only the owner, and whether the group steers people toward paid offers.
