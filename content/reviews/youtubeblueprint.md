# The YouTube Blueprint Review: A Free Route to YouTube Growth

Calling something the straightest-line path to YouTube growth is a bold framing. The YouTube Blueprint has 2,498 members, no listed price, and a description that asks you to master the game of YouTube.

## What's Actually Inside

The listing is a single sentence, so what is inside is mostly unknown. It promises YouTube growth and a direct route, but it does not say whether lessons cover thumbnails, titles, scripting, retention or monetisation. We will not assume any.

No price is listed, so we treat it as free. The dataset records a 50 percent affiliate rate and an MRR badge labelled clover, which points to the owner earning recurring revenue from the platform, probably from a paid offer. That suggests the free community may connect to a paid product, which is common and worth knowing before you join.

Membership is steady, rising from 2,490 to 2,498 in recent snapshots.

The owner shows high activity: 22 active days in the last 30 and 82 in the last 90. Regular presence tends to mean questions get answered.

A good test of any YouTube advice is whether it is aimed at small channels. Strategies that work at scale do not always help a channel with no audience.

If you join, look at how the classroom is organised and whether free material is complete enough to act on by itself. A good free community gives you a usable starting point and is honest about what sits behind a paywall, instead of leaving every useful answer for a later purchase.
