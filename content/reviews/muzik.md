# Müzik Üniversitesi Review 2026: Free Turkish Music Learning Community on Skool

Turkish speakers looking to pick up an instrument or improve their singing have a dedicated home in Müzik Üniversitesi (Ücretsiz), a free community that has grown past 8,900 members. The description is short but clear: "Gitar, şarkı söyleme ve tüm müzik enstrümanlarını öğren," which translates to "Learn guitar, singing, and all music instruments."

## What's Actually Inside

The description names guitar and singing explicitly before broadening out to "all music instruments," which suggests the community may have started with a guitar-and-vocals focus before expanding its scope, or simply wants to signal it welcomes learners of any instrument. Either way, the core promise is music education in a general sense rather than a single-instrument deep dive. No Skool category is listed for this community, so there's no additional platform-level signal beyond the Turkish-language description itself. Given the "Ücretsiz" (free) tag baked directly into the display name, cost transparency appears to be a deliberate part of the community's branding, likely to distinguish it from a paid counterpart or to reassure prospective members upfront.

The membership history shows steady, consistent growth, moving from roughly 8,917 to 8,938 members across the sampled dates, which suggests an actively growing group rather than a stagnant one. For a hobby-focused, language-specific community like this, that kind of ongoing growth is a reasonable sign that word of mouth among Turkish-speaking musicians is still bringing in new members.

## Who Runs It

Deniz Sayman is listed as the community's owner in the platform data. No further background is available from the data reviewed here.

## Pros

- Free to join, with that fact emphasized directly in the community's name.
- Broad instructional scope covering guitar, singing, and other instruments, useful for anyone still deciding what to learn.
- A membership base approaching 9,000 suggests meaningful activity for a Turkish-language music community.

## Cons

- The description doesn't specify skill level, so it's unclear whether lessons target complete beginners or more advanced musicians.
- Covering "all music instruments" alongside guitar and singing specifically is a broad promise, and the actual depth on any one instrument isn't detailed.
- Non-Turkish speakers will face a language barrier, since both the name and description are in Turkish.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Turkish-speaking beginners interested in learning guitar, singing, or another instrument who want a free, community-based starting point.

## Who Should Skip It

Non-Turkish speakers, and advanced musicians looking for specialized, instrument-specific instruction rather than a broad, general music-learning community.
