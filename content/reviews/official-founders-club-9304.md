## Who It's a Good Fit For

Hindi-speaking beginners who have little capital but a serious intention to start a business, and who are willing to pay for a structured, community-supported approach.

People who want to sample the content for free first may prefer one of the many free communities in the same space. Experienced founders will probably find a beginner program too basic. Anyone unwilling to pay upfront for an annual plan without seeing the lessons should ask about refunds or trial options before committing.
