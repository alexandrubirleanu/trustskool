# Oracle Connections Review 2026: Is $7/Month Worth It for Oracle Card Creators?

Oracle Connections costs $7 a month and has 4,546 members. It positions itself for "Soulful Entrepreneurs, Oracle Cards Lovers + Creators," and its second line says it is about bringing traffic through connections with no content, then growing and scaling Skool using mini-projects.

## What's Actually Inside

Two audiences are named. One is spiritual or soulful entrepreneurs, the other is people who love or create oracle cards. The method described is distinctive: grow traffic through connections rather than constantly producing content, and scale a Skool community through small "mini-projects." That is a growth angle aimed at people who dislike posting every day.

The details are thin, though. The description does not explain what a mini-project is, how connection-based traffic works in practice, or whether there are courses, calls or templates. It also leaves open how much of the community is about oracle cards themselves versus building a Skool business around that interest. The "Top 1%" label is a self-description with no supporting data in the record.

At $7 monthly the price is low, so the main cost is time, not money. It is reasonable to treat it as a trial of the method.

## Who Runs It

The owner listed is Katya McEwen. The data supplies no biography or results, so anything beyond that should be judged from her posts and how members engage with them.

## Pros

- A $7 monthly price is easy to test and cancel if it is not useful.
- A clear and unusual niche that combines oracle card interest with online business growth.
- The connection-first, no-content approach may appeal to people who dislike constant posting.
- 4,546 members indicates a sizable community for such a specific audience.

## Cons

- "Mini-projects" and "traffic through connections" are not explained, so the actual method is unclear.
- "Top 1%" is a claim, not a figure, and nothing in the record verifies it.
- The blend of spiritual and business audiences may leave either side wanting more.
- The description does not list courses, live sessions or deliverables.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Oracle card readers, creators and spiritual entrepreneurs who want to build a Skool community through relationships rather than daily content. Low price makes it suitable for someone exploring the idea cautiously.

## Who Should Skip It

People with no interest in oracle cards or spirituality, who may feel out of place. Also anyone wanting a rigorous, data-driven growth system, or a detailed curriculum they can inspect before paying. If you want content-heavy marketing tactics, the explicit "no content" focus is the opposite of that.
