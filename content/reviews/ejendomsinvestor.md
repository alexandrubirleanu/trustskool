# Ejendomsforløbet (Lite) Review 2026: Free Danish Community for First-Time Landlords

How do you buy your first rental property without drowning in financing jargon and paperwork? That is the exact problem Ejendomsforløbet (Lite) positions itself to solve. It is a free, Danish-language Skool community with 7,432 members, built around one clear promise translated from its own description: "Learn how to buy your first rental property. Step-by-step!"

## What's Actually Inside

The description is short but pointed. It is not a general real estate market commentary group, it is aimed squarely at Danish speakers who have not yet bought a rental property (udlejningsejendom) and want a structured sequence to follow. "Step-by-step" implies members move through defined stages rather than absorbing scattered tips, likely covering things like identifying a suitable property, assessing financing, running the numbers on cash flow, and closing the purchase. The listing itself does not spell out the specific stages, financing mechanics, or whether guidance comes through video, live calls, or written material, so prospective members should expect to find that structure once inside rather than from the pitch alone.

## Cons

- The description does not detail financing specifics, loan-to-value expectations, or Danish rental law, so members will need to verify those details inside the community rather than from the listing.
- It is unclear whether the "step-by-step" system is delivered as a self-paced course, live coaching, or a mix of both.
- No independent reviews of this community were found beyond its own Skool page.

## Who It's a Good Fit For
