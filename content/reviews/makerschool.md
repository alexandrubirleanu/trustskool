# Maker School: AI Automation Review 2026: Is the 90-Day First Client Promise Worth It?

Few Skool descriptions lead with a guarantee, but this one does. Maker School: AI Automation says it will help you get your first client for an AI automation business in 90 days, or your money back. The community has 2,176 members and is listed in the money category.

That is a bold claim. It comes from the community's own description and is not verified by TrustSkool, so read it as a marketing promise rather than an established result.

## Pricing

This is the main point to clarify before joining. The listing's price field is empty, which would normally suggest a free community. However, the description itself states "$184/mo." The two do not match, and the data does not explain why. The most likely reading is that membership is paid at the rate named in the description, but you should confirm the actual price and the terms of the money-back guarantee on the Skool page before signing up.

## What's Actually Inside

The description lists three components:

- **Daily AI coaching.** It does not say whether this is live, recorded, written feedback, or an AI tool doing the coaching.
- **AI templates.** Presumably ready-made automation builds you can adapt for clients, though the specific tools are not named.
- **Tool savings.** The description claims members get more than $30K saved in tools, which likely refers to discounts or perks. The figure is the community's own and is not broken down.

The overall goal is clear: take someone from zero to a first paying client in AI automation. That is a narrower and more practical promise than general "learn AI" communities. What is missing is any detail on the curriculum, how sales and outreach are taught, or what counts as qualifying for the refund.

## Who Runs It

The listing names Nick Saraev as the owner. No further background is available in the data, so this review does not speculate about his track record. The listing also shows a 20% affiliate commission and a "diamond" revenue status in TrustSkool's data, which points to a paid offer, but neither of those says anything about the quality of the teaching.

## Pros

- A specific, measurable outcome (a first client) rather than vague promises.
- A stated money-back guarantee, which lowers risk if the terms are fair.
- Daily coaching suggests frequent support rather than a static course.
- Templates can shorten the learning curve for building client work.

## Cons

- Price is not set in the listing and only appears in the description, so the real cost needs checking.
- At $184 per month, if that figure holds, it is a meaningful ongoing expense for someone without income from the skill yet.
- Guarantee conditions are not described.
- The $30K tool savings claim is unverified.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

People who are serious about starting an AI automation service business and want structure, frequent coaching, and templates to get their first client. It suits those who can afford a monthly fee while they build, and who respond well to a deadline-driven program.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone looking for a free or low-cost introduction to AI tools, hobbyists who do not want to sell services, and people who cannot comfortably carry a monthly subscription for several months. If you need clear refund terms before paying, get them in writing first.
