# Google Ads Skool Review 2026: Free Community for Campaign Feedback and Support

Getting direct feedback on a live ad campaign is harder to find for free than generic advice, which is the specific angle Google Ads Skool leans on. The free Skool community, with over 5,090 members, promises to help members "launch, optimize, and scale winning Google Ads campaigns from scratch," plus "feedback on your campaigns and direct support."

## What's Actually Inside

The description covers the full campaign lifecycle in general terms, launching from scratch, optimizing what's running, and scaling what works, which suggests content aimed at both newcomers and people with existing campaigns to improve. The mention of "feedback on your campaigns and direct support" is notable because it implies some level of individualized review rather than purely generic lesson content, though the description doesn't specify whether that feedback comes from the community owner, other members, or a mix, nor how quickly or reliably it's provided. It falls under Skool's money category, consistent with a paid-advertising skill focus. Membership grew steadily across the tracked snapshots, from 5,090 up to 5,099 members.

## Who Runs It

Rob Andolina is listed as the owner of this community on Skool. No further independent background or credentials are available in the data reviewed here.

## Pros

- Free access to campaign feedback and support is valuable in a niche, paid ads management, where consulting and agency fees are often expensive.
- Covering the full lifecycle, launch, optimize, scale, means the community isn't narrowly limited to only beginners or only advanced advertisers.
- Direct feedback on individual campaigns, if delivered consistently, is a more actionable format than generic video lessons alone.

## Cons

- The description doesn't specify how feedback is delivered, whether that's live calls, async posts, or something else, or how much individual attention a given member can realistically expect in a community of this size.
- No independent reviews of this community were found beyond its own Skool listing, so claims about direct support can't be verified from outside sources.
- Google Ads platform features and best practices change frequently, and the description doesn't indicate how current or actively updated the content is kept.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Advertisers and business owners running or planning to run Google Ads campaigns who want a free space for feedback and troubleshooting alongside foundational strategy content.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone looking for guidance on other ad platforms, like Meta or TikTok ads, should look elsewhere, since this community is specifically framed around Google Ads. People who need guaranteed one-on-one attention rather than community-based feedback may also want to confirm the actual support model before joining.
