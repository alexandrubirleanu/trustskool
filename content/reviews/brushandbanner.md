# Miniature Painting School Review 2026: Is $15 a Month Worth It for Feedback on Your Minis?

Miniature painting is one of those hobbies where progress comes faster when someone else looks at your work. Miniature Painting School leans directly into that idea, describing itself as an educational environment for painters who want feedback, structured lessons, guides, and a supportive group of like-minded students. It has 2,424 members and charges $15 USD per month.

## What's Actually Inside

The description names four things, and they are fairly concrete for a Skool listing:

1. **Feedback** on members' painting, which is the part most hobbyists struggle to find elsewhere.
2. **Structured lessons**, suggesting an ordered curriculum rather than a loose pile of videos.
3. **Guides**, presumably written or visual references members can return to.
4. **A community of students**, framed as supportive and like-minded.

The word "school" and the repeated "students" framing tell you the intent is teaching, not just showing off finished models. What the listing does not say is which games or scales are covered, how feedback is delivered (written comments, video critiques, or live sessions), or how often new lessons arrive. Those are fair questions to ask before committing to a recurring payment.

## Who Runs It

Mat Saparito is listed as the community's owner. There is no further background on him in the data available, so this review doesn't speculate about his painting experience or teaching history. You can judge the instruction quality yourself once inside.

## Pros

- A clear, specific promise: feedback plus structured lessons, not just a social feed.
- $15 a month is a modest price for a hobby education community, roughly the cost of a couple of paint pots.
- 2,424 members means there should be plenty of other painters sharing work and comparing techniques.
- The educational framing suits people who want to improve deliberately, not just chat.

## Cons

- It is a recurring monthly cost, which adds up if you only paint occasionally.
- The listing doesn't specify how much personal feedback each member can expect, which matters at this membership size.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Hobbyist miniature painters who have hit a plateau and want structured lessons alongside critique of their own work. It also suits newer painters who prefer learning in a defined order instead of piecing together free tutorials.

## Who Should Skip It

Painters who just want a free place to post photos and chat, or who already get regular critique from a local club. If you only pick up a brush a few times a year, a monthly subscription may not make sense.
