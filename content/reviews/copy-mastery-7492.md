A copywriting launch usually has several moving parts. You need a writing sample or two, a clear offer, a way to find your first client, and a price that is neither embarrassing nor out of reach. A community can help with each of these through feedback and accountability, although the listing does not say whether this one is structured that way.

Freelancing also brings the less glamorous side: late payments, scope creep, and uneven months. The word freedom in the pitch is appealing, but it is worth asking how the community prepares members for those realities, and whether members work with French-speaking clients, international ones, or both.
