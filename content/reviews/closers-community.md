# Closers Community™ Review 2026: Free French Sales Community for Aspiring Closers

Closers Community™ is a French-language Skool group with about 3,350 members and no listed price. Its description, translated, says: "We help you become a closer who earns five figures a month by selling without a script and without the nonsense." That is a strong promise, so it is worth separating what the listing says from what it can show.

## What's Actually Inside

The description names one target outcome, a closer earning five figures monthly, and one method, selling without a script. A closer, in this context, is a sales professional who handles the final stage of a sale, usually on calls. Beyond that, the listing gives no breakdown of lessons, live coaching, role plays, job boards or any other component. It does not say what currency the five figures refers to, how long the path takes, or whether any member has reached that level.

The tone of the text, direct and a little irreverent, hints at a community that positions itself against rigid sales scripts. Whether the inside matches that tone is something a new member would learn after joining. The "™" in the name suggests a branded program, though the data does not describe what the brand includes.

## Who Runs It

The listing names Valentin Nourrit as the owner of this community. That is the only information about the person included in the data behind this review, so no background, credentials or track record are described here.

## Pros

- Free to join, which lowers the risk of testing a sales-career community.
- A specific target role (closer) rather than a generic "make money online" promise.
- French-language, useful for people who want to sell to French-speaking markets.
- A sizeable group of roughly 3,350 members to learn alongside.

## Cons

- The five-figure monthly income claim is not backed by any data in the listing, and it should not be read as typical.
- No detail on training format, coaching or support.
- "Without a script" is a method claim with no explanation of what replaces the script.
- Free communities of this kind sometimes lead to paid offers, and the listing does not say whether this one does.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

French speakers considering a move into remote or commission-based sales who want a free look at how this community frames the closing role, and who are comfortable verifying income claims for themselves.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who expects guaranteed income should skip it, as no such guarantee exists in the data and none should be assumed. People who want a documented curriculum, or who do not read French, will likely find the listing too thin to act on.
