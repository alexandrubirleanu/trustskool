The listed price is $3 per month. We don't know if there are other tiers or paid add-ons behind the links mentioned in the description.

The low price makes this an easy one to sample, but it is still worth checking what sits behind the links in the description and whether any recommendations steer members toward products sold by the owner or by partners. Recipe sharing from members is only as reliable as the people posting, so cross-check dilution guidance with reputable safety sources.

Jenny Williams is listed as the owner. The data does not tell us about her training or experience, so we do not claim any. Essential oil guidance varies widely in reliability, so it helps to know who is giving it.

## Pros

- A very low listed price of $3 per month.
- A mix of recipes, mini courses and peer connection.
- 2,737 members suggests an active interest in the topic.

## Cons

- Health benefits of essential oils and "toxin-free" living are often overstated in wellness spaces, and no evidence is shown here.
- The description sends readers to external links without saying what they lead to.
- No details on the mini courses.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

With a listed price of only $3 per month, it works as a low-risk way to sample the topic and the group. Hobbyists curious about DIY essential oil blends and low-toxin household habits who enjoy a friendly group and treat the advice as lifestyle ideas.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone seeking medical treatment, or with pets, young children, pregnancy or allergies, should consult a professional, since essential oils are not safe for everyone. People wanting evidence-based health teaching should look elsewhere. Enjoy it as a lifestyle community, and keep medical decisions with qualified health professionals. At this price, the downside of trying it is small, which is the most solid point in its favor.
