# Online-Video-Kurs Community Review: Selling Courses Without Big Reach

Plenty of people have knowledge worth teaching, but the camera, editing and course platform stop them. Online-Video-Kurs Community, with 2,608 members, addresses that in German. Its three-line pitch is to create your online course, sell "without reach and tech chaos," and let the community help.

## What's Actually Inside

The description gives three promises: build a course, sell it without a big audience, and keep the technical side manageable. It does not describe lessons, software recommendations or live help sessions, so those remain unknown.

No price is listed, so we treat it as free. The "sell without reach" claim is the owner's own framing, and the listing offers no figures to support it.

For a course-creation community, the useful test is whether members actually publish. Look for posts showing finished courses, early sales and feedback on offers, not just questions about gear.

To judge it fairly, imagine your first course and ask what you would need: a topic, an outline, a recording setup, a place to host it and a way to attract buyers. See whether the community addresses each step or only one. Look at member introductions to find out how many are at the idea stage versus already selling. A large group of 2,608 can offer feedback on outlines and titles quickly, which is often the most valuable help for new creators.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

German-speaking experts, coaches and trainers who want to turn their knowledge into a video course and need guidance on the tech and the sale.

## Who Should Skip It

People who need hands-on editing training, non-German speakers, or anyone expecting a ready-made audience. Experienced course sellers may find little new.

Test it with one small course idea first. If the guidance helps you ship something within a few weeks, it has earned your time.
