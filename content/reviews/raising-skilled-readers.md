# Raising Skilled Readers Review 2026: A Free Parent Education Community

Parents worried about a child's reading progress are the clear audience for Raising Skilled Readers, a free Skool community that has attracted 8,135 members. The description promises to teach parents "how to raise a skilled reader, without battles and uncertainty," pairing that with a promise of "expert support and parent education."

## What's Actually Inside

The phrase "without battles" is doing real work here: it signals that the community is at least partly addressing the emotional and behavioral side of reading struggles, not just phonics or curriculum choice. That's a meaningfully specific angle compared to a generic "help your kid read better" pitch, since it suggests attention to the friction that often comes up between parents and reluctant or struggling readers. "Parent education" as a stated focus also clarifies who the actual audience is: this is built for the adults guiding a child's reading, not for the children themselves or for classroom teachers.

The description mentions "expert support," which implies some structured guidance beyond peer conversation, though the listing doesn't specify whether that means live sessions, a specific reading methodology, screened credentials, or something else. The membership figure held essentially flat across the platform's short tracked window, which doesn't tell us much on its own but is worth noting alongside the raw member count.

## Who Runs It

The platform lists Doug Evans as the community's owner. Beyond that name, no further public background or credentials appear in the data reviewed here, so nothing else about this person can be confirmed from the listing.

## Pros

- Free to join, which matters for parents who may already be spending on tutoring, curriculum, or other reading support.
- A membership base above 8,100 suggests this is a well-established resource rather than a small, unproven group.
- The "without battles and uncertainty" framing addresses the parent-child dynamic directly, a more specific angle than most generic reading-help content.

## Cons

- The description doesn't name a specific reading methodology, curriculum, or the credentials behind the "expert support" it promises, so that's worth confirming before joining if it matters to you.
- No independent reviews of Raising Skilled Readers beyond its own Skool listing were found, so this assessment relies entirely on the public description and platform data.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Parents looking for a free community focused specifically on the emotional and practical challenges of teaching a child to read.

## Who Should Skip It
