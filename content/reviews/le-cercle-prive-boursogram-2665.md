## What's Actually Inside

The French description translates roughly as: "In this private community, I share with you all the educational resources that will allow you to adopt a real profitable strategy on the stock market." Note the first-person voice. This reads as one person sharing material, not a panel of experts.

What that material is, we can't say. The listing doesn't mention video lessons, live analysis, trade alerts, portfolios or a classroom. The phrase "real profitable strategy" is the owner's promise.
