In the digital products world, MRR usually refers to master resell rights, meaning a product that buyers are allowed to resell themselves. The listing does not define the term, so it is worth confirming what it means here before joining. The community has 1,983 members and no price on its listing, so it appears to be free to enter.

## What's Actually Inside

According to the description, SWC aims to teach two things:

1. **The fundamentals of digital marketing for complete beginners.** This suggests foundational material, likely covering things like social media posting, audiences, and basic sales concepts.
2. **Multiple ways to make income online.** The listing does not name which methods are included.

The "MRR course" label is the most important detail. If it does mean master resell rights, the course itself may be something members can buy and then resell, which is a common model in this niche. The listing does not clarify this, and it does not mention any price for the course itself, even though the community entry is free. There may be paid parts that are not shown in the data.
