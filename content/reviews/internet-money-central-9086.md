# Content Creator University Review 2026: Free Training or a Funnel to a Premium Group?

Content Creator University makes its goal clear right away: "Helping you make your first $100k as a Content Creator!" It is free to join and lists 2,373 members. Its description also points to something else, a separate premium Skool community at a different address, which is worth understanding before you sign up.

## What's Actually Inside

The core promise is income from content creation, framed around a first $100k. That figure is the community's own target, not a verified result, and how long it might take or how many members have reached it is not stated anywhere in the data.

The second half of the description is a direct call to "Join My Premium Skool," followed by a link to a community called leveragelab. That tells us this free group sits alongside a paid or premium offer. It is a common setup on Skool: a free community attracts people, and a premium one holds the deeper material or closer access. The listing does not say what is included in the free version versus the premium one.

Notice, too, that the community's URL slug ("internet-money-central") does not match its display name. That may simply mean it was renamed at some point; the data does not explain it.

## Who Runs It

Jason Cooperson is listed as the owner. No further background is available in the data, so there is nothing here to verify about his own creator income or audience size.

## Pros

- Free to join, so you can see the owner's approach before considering anything paid.
- A clear, single goal (monetizing content) rather than a vague "make money online" theme.
- Upfront about the premium community, so the upsell is not hidden.

## Cons

- The $100k framing is an aspirational income claim with no supporting data.
- The free group's own content is not described at all; the description spends half its space promoting the premium one.
- Unclear what separates free from premium, which makes it hard to know how much value stays on the free side.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Aspiring or early-stage creators who want a free look at one person's take on monetization and do not mind being pointed toward a premium option. If you already suspect you might want paid coaching later, starting in the free group is a sensible way to test the fit.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who dislikes being marketed to inside a community, or who wants a complete free curriculum with no upsell, may find the setup frustrating. Creators who are already earning well beyond the first-income stage are probably not the target audience either.
