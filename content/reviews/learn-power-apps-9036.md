## Cons

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Office workers, analysts and aspiring citizen developers who want to build internal apps and prefer asking real people over reading documentation alone.

## Who Should Skip It

People who only want a quick introduction, who can learn from free official material, or who plan to use other platforms. Traditional software engineers wanting deep custom code may find a low-code community limiting.

Before paying $27, scan the feed for questions that match your own use cases, such as forms, approvals or data connections, and see whether they get clear answers. Check how recent the newest posts are and whether the owner replies himself. Monthly billing means you can cancel if the material turns out to be outdated or too basic for your level, so a single month works as a low-risk trial.
