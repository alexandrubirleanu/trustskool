# Fitness Coaching Community Review 2026: Can a Free German Group Help Coaches Scale?

Most fitness communities are for people who want to get fit. This one is for people who get others fit and want to build a better business doing it. Fitness Coaching Community operates in German, is free to join, and lists 2,286 members.

Its description, translated, says it is the community for fitness coaches who care about four things: freedom, fun, happy clients, and revenue. It then adds, with some emphasis, "a lot of revenue," pointing to profits above €10,000 per month.

## What's Actually Inside

The four values give a sense of the community's angle. "Freedom" and "fun" suggest a lifestyle-business mindset, likely online or flexible coaching rather than gym-floor employment. "Happy clients" puts some weight on service quality, and "revenue" makes it clear this is a business group first.

The €10,000+ monthly profit figure is the community's stated aspiration or benchmark. It is not verified in the data, and it is unclear whether it describes the owner, typical members, or a goal. Readers should treat it as marketing until they see evidence inside.

The listing does not describe specific content such as client acquisition training, pricing frameworks, program design, or live calls. Because everything runs in German, non-German speakers would struggle to follow discussions.

## Who Runs It

Jannis Neumann is listed as the owner. No further background is available in the data, so this review does not comment on his coaching business or credentials.

## Pros

- Free, with a clear audience: working fitness coaches, not general fitness fans.
- Balances business goals with lifestyle and client satisfaction, at least in its stated values.
- A German-language space can be a real advantage for coaches serving the DACH market.

## Cons

- The €10,000+ profit figure is an unverified income claim.
- No information about the actual content, structure, or support offered.
- Limited usefulness for anyone not comfortable reading and discussing in German.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

German-speaking fitness coaches, especially those running or planning an online coaching business, who want a free community centered on growing revenue without giving up freedom or client quality. Coaches who are early in their business may find the peer network helpful for benchmarking.

## Who Should Skip It

People looking for workout plans or personal fitness advice are not the audience. Coaches who need English-language content, or who are put off by income-focused positioning, should probably look elsewhere.
