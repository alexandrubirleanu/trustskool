# AdSuccess Academy (Free) Review: Learning Google and Facebook Ads for Nothing

AdSuccess Academy (Free) is an English-language community with 2,911 members that promises to teach Google Ads and Facebook Ads. The word "Free" sits right in the name, and the description repeats it. The core question is how much depth a free offer can deliver, and the listing leaves that open.

## What's Actually Inside

According to the description, members can "learn to create successful Google Ads & Facebook Ads campaigns and improve current ads for free." That covers two things: building new campaigns on those two platforms and improving ads that already exist. The listing does not break this into modules, name any instructors beyond the owner, or describe formats such as videos, live reviews or templates.

No price is listed, which matches the "free" wording in the name and description. The promise of "successful" campaigns is the community's own framing, and we have no results to back it.

A practical way to judge a free ads community is to check whether the lessons show real account screenshots, explain budgets and targeting choices, and cover measurement, or whether they stay at a general level. The best free material usually teaches you to read your own data instead of copying a formula.

## Who Runs It

Erdi Bus is listed as the owner. The data does not explain his experience running ad accounts, so we are not going to attribute any. Ad platforms reward real spend-tested experience, so it is worth looking for evidence of that before you trust specific advice.

## Pros

- Free by its own description, so you can look at the material at no cost.
- Covers two of the most widely used paid ad platforms.
- Open to both new advertisers and people who want to fix existing ads.

## Cons

- Many free communities use the free material to steer members toward paid offers, and the listing does not say whether that is the case here.
- No curriculum or tool details are given.
- Ad platform interfaces and rules change often, so content may age quickly.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Small business owners, freelancers and marketing beginners who want an introduction to Google and Facebook advertising without paying, and who will test what they learn on small budgets.

## Who Should Skip It

Experienced media buyers will probably find it too basic, as will anyone expecting a guaranteed path to profitable campaigns. People wanting one-to-one account audits or formal certification should look elsewhere. Keep ad budgets small while you learn, because lessons only prove themselves against your own results. Judge the community by what you can apply, not by its name alone.
