# Barber Mastermind Review 2026: Free Skool Community for Growing a Barbershop

Barber Mastermind is aimed at a rare niche on Skool: people who run, or want to run, a barbershop. The community is free, has about 3,430 members and is in English. Its description promises strategies to grow a barbershop to $1,000,000 and beyond, keep barber chairs full and hire top barbers.

## What's Actually Inside

Three goals are listed: revenue growth, filling chairs and hiring. Each corresponds to a real operational challenge for shop owners, which makes the scope easy to understand. The description frames the million-dollar mark as a target, not a result, and it does not say how many members have reached it.

What the listing omits is the format. There is no information about whether content is video lessons, live Q&A, templates for hiring or pricing, or a discussion board. It also does not say whether the strategies fit single-chair operators, booth renters or multi-chair shops, which are different businesses. Members should expect to find these answers after joining.

The data lists no price, so the community is treated as free. With about 3,430 members it is large for such a specific trade. Business ideas for a shop depend on local factors such as rent, licensing, clientele and competition, so strategies from a national or online audience may need adjusting. A good test after joining is whether discussions include real numbers, such as chair rental rates or booking rates, rather than only broad motivation.
