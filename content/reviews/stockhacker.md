# 炒股黑客 (Stock Hacker) Review 2026: A Free 7-Day Prop Firm Community for Chinese Traders

Seven days to understand Prop Firm trading is a bold promise, and it's the entire pitch behind 炒股黑客 (roughly "Stock Hacker"), a free Chinese-language Skool community that has grown to 5,838 members. The description is short and direct: 幫助朝九晚五上班族，在 7 天內弄懂 Prop Firm與交易的免費中文社群🔥, "a free Chinese community helping 9-to-5 workers understand Prop Firm and trading within 7 days."

## What's Actually Inside

Prop firm trading, where a trader gets funded capital from a proprietary trading firm after passing an evaluation, is a specific and fairly technical niche within retail trading, distinct from just "learn to trade stocks." The description targets office workers with regular jobs (朝九晚五, literally "9 to 5"), suggesting the content is built around limited time and a fast on-ramp rather than a slow, comprehensive trading education. Beyond the 7-day framing and the Prop Firm focus, the listing doesn't specify which firms, evaluation types, or asset classes (forex, futures, stocks) are covered.

## Pros

- Free to join, which lowers the barrier for people just exploring whether prop firm trading fits their schedule and risk tolerance.
- A narrow, specific niche (prop firm trading) rather than a vague "learn to trade" pitch.
- Nearly 5,900 members suggests meaningful traction within the Chinese-speaking trading community.
- The "9-to-5 worker" framing signals content pitched at people with limited daily time, not full-time day traders.

## Cons

- The 7-day promise is aggressive for a topic, funded trading evaluations, that typically requires proving consistent risk management over weeks or months, and the description doesn't explain what "understanding" it in 7 days actually delivers.
- No specific prop firms, evaluation criteria, or asset classes are named, so it's unclear how directly actionable the content is.
- No independent reviews of this community were found beyond its own Skool listing, and trading-focused communities in general carry real financial risk that a review like this can't verify.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Mandarin or Chinese-speaking office workers curious about prop firm trading who want a free, fast introduction before committing time or money elsewhere, and who are comfortable doing further due diligence on any specific firm or strategy the community recommends.

## Who Should Skip It

Non-Chinese speakers will find the language barrier immediate, since the community operates in Chinese. Anyone expecting a comprehensive, risk-managed trading education, or looking for a slower, more thorough introduction than a 7-day framing implies, should look for alternatives that make fewer speed-based promises.
