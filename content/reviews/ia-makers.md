# IA MAKERS Review 2026: Is $49/Month Worth It for Monetizing Social Media with AI?

That puts it in the middle of the market for paid creator education: more than a casual group, far below a high-ticket mastermind. The pitch is to monetize YouTube, Facebook and TikTok from anywhere in the world using artificial intelligence, and it adds a bold line: "we are the community with the most student results."

## What's Actually Inside

Three platforms are named, which tells us the focus is on short and long video distribution and the revenue those platforms can pay or enable. Artificial intelligence is the production engine, though the description does not say which tools or what kind of content (faceless videos, voiceovers, clips, automation). "From anywhere in the world" signals a location-independent angle aimed at Spanish speakers across different countries.

The results claim deserves care. "The community with the most student results" is a comparative statement without any numbers, examples or source in the listing. It may well be true to the owner, but a prospective member should ask to see specific, checkable cases before treating it as fact. The description also does not mention course modules, live calls, templates or support, so those are worth confirming against the $49 monthly price.

Monthly billing at least keeps the commitment flexible, and you can judge value after a month. Just check the cancellation terms yourself.

The owner is listed as Fabio Villanueva. The data available does not include a biography or channel results, so nothing more can be said about his background. His public content would be the best place to see whether the strategies work in practice.

## Pros

- Spanish-language, serving a market that often gets English leftovers.
- Month-to-month pricing, so you can leave if it does not deliver.

## Cons

- The "most results" claim is unsupported by any numbers in the listing.
- No tools, curriculum or format details are given for the $49 monthly fee.
- Platform monetization rules change, and AI-generated content faces policy scrutiny on some platforms.
- Paying every month adds up over a year to $588 if you stay.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Spanish-speaking creators who want a guided, AI-driven path to earning from short and long video and are willing to pay monthly for structure. It fits those who will ask for proof of results before committing.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who expects guaranteed income, or who cannot spare time to produce content consistently. People wanting free or very low-cost exploration may prefer free communities, and non-Spanish speakers will not benefit from the language.
