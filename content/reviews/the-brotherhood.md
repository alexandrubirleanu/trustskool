# The Brotherhood Review 2026: Free Faith-Based Community for Husbands and Fathers

Some faith communities stay broad, aiming at "spiritual growth" in the abstract. The Brotherhood narrows its focus to specific roles: it is "rooted in real-life experience and scripture" and describes itself as providing "practical strategies to transform your role as a believer, husband, father, and leader." The community is free to join and has 7,228 members.

## What's Actually Inside

The four roles named in the description, believer, husband, father, leader, give a concrete sense of who this is built for and what it addresses: men navigating faith alongside marriage, parenting, and leadership responsibilities, rather than a general self-improvement or spirituality group open to all comers. The phrase "real-life experience and scripture" suggests content draws on both personal testimony or lived example and biblical teaching, positioned as practical rather than purely theological. The description does not detail the specific format, whether that is discussion threads, live calls, structured teaching modules, or accountability groups, so members should expect to discover that structure once inside.
