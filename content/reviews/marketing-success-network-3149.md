# Leverage Leader Review 2026: Free Social Media Growth Community for Service Creators

Leverage Leader is a free Skool community with 6,960 members aimed squarely at service-based creators who want to grow using social media, with a specific revenue target named directly in the pitch: scaling to $10,000 or more per month. John McKay is named as the person behind it.

## What's Actually Inside

The description names a specific audience, "service creators", and a specific channel, social media, rather than a generic "grow your business" pitch. It breaks the value proposition into three concrete steps: reach more people, get more engagement, and scale revenue to a stated $10,000+/month threshold. That revenue figure is a specific, checkable claim about outcomes, which is more concrete than most free growth communities offer, though the listing doesn't say which social platforms are the focus, what "service creator" specifically means, coaches, consultants, freelancers, or agencies, or what content, paid ads, or funnel strategies are actually taught to hit that number.

## Pros

- Names a specific audience, service creators, and a specific revenue target, $10,000+/month, rather than vague growth language.
- Free to join, so there's no cost to test whether the approach fits your situation.
- Breaks the pitch into a clear three-step framework: reach, engagement, then revenue scale.
- Nearly 7,000 members suggests real demand for this specific angle.

## Cons

The $10,000+/month figure is presented as an outcome without any explanation of typical timelines, starting points, or what percentage of members actually reach it, so it should be read as an aspirational target rather than a guaranteed result. The description also doesn't specify which social platforms or content strategies are covered, or how John McKay's own experience with service creators informs the approach. We found no independent reviews of this community beyond its own Skool listing.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Coaches, consultants, freelancers, or other service-based creators who want a free community focused specifically on social media growth and revenue scaling, and who are comfortable treating the $10,000+/month figure as a goal rather than a promise.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone selling physical products rather than services may find the "service creator" framing doesn't fit. Those who want specific platform strategies, say, only short-form video or only LinkedIn, spelled out before joining should also look for more detail first.
