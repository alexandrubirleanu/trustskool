# Aviator Intelligence Review 2026: Career Community for Pilots on Skool

Few professions have a career ladder as defined as commercial aviation, and Aviator Intelligence is built around it. Its 2,806 members are pilots at various stages, and the description promises guidance "from CFI to the majors, and every step in between."

## What's Actually Inside

The full text reads: "Helping pilots navigate their aviation careers. From CFI to the majors, and every step in between, we guide you through the journey to your dream job." CFI refers to certified flight instructor, usually an early step for building flight hours, and "the majors" refers to large airlines.

So the scope is the whole progression of a pilot's working life. What isn't spelled out is the delivery. We can't see whether there are interview prep materials, airline hiring updates, mentor calls, networking threads or resume help. The description speaks as "we," but the data names only one owner.

## Who Runs It

Dustin Benker is listed as the owner. The data does not say what his flying background is, and we won't speculate on it.

## Pros

- Highly specific audience and goal, which tends to produce more relevant discussion than generic career groups.
- Covers multiple career stages, so it may remain useful as you progress.
- No listed price in the data reviewed, which may mean an easy way in. Check the join page to confirm.
- Membership near 2,800 for a specialised profession is a decent signal.

## Cons

- The listing offers no specifics about resources, so what you receive is unclear.
- Airline hiring conditions change, and we could not verify how current any guidance is.
- No independent reviews or placement outcomes were found, so "dream job" remains an aspiration.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Student pilots, flight instructors and regional pilots planning a route to larger airlines, particularly those who want to compare notes with others on the same path. It also fits people who value peer insight on timelines and decisions.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone looking for flight training, ground school or exam preparation will not find that described here. Those not working toward a pilot career gain little from the focus. And no community replaces official regulatory guidance or advice from your own employer or training provider. If you do join, compare what members say about hiring timelines and requirements against official airline and regulator sources, because those details shift and peer anecdotes can age quickly.
