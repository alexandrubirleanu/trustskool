# YouTube Okulu Review 2026: Is This Free Turkish YouTube School Worth a Look?

"YouTube Okulu" means "YouTube School" in Turkish, and the name tells you most of what you need to know. This is a free, Turkish-language Skool community with 2,378 members, built for people who want to learn how YouTube works.

The description is short. Translated, it says that in this community you will be able to learn everything about YouTube and find answers to your questions. That is a broad promise, so the rest of this review focuses on what can reasonably be taken from it and what remains unknown.

## What's Actually Inside

Two things are stated. First, learning: the group positions itself as a place to learn "everything" about YouTube. Second, answers: members can bring questions and expect to have them addressed. Together those suggest a mix of educational material and a Q&A style discussion space.

The listing does not break down which YouTube topics are covered. There's no mention of thumbnails, scripting, editing, monetization, Shorts, or analytics specifically, so you shouldn't assume any one of those is a focus. "Everything" is a claim from the description, not a verified syllabus.

## Who Runs It

Erkan Kolcu is listed as the owner. The data includes no further background on him, so this review makes no assumptions about his own channel or results.

## Pros

- Free, which lowers the barrier for new creators.
- Built for Turkish speakers, a language that has far fewer YouTube education resources than English.
- Explicit focus on answering members' questions, useful when you are stuck on something specific.
- A base of 2,378 members gives you a pool of fellow Turkish creators to learn alongside.

## Cons

- The description is very brief, so the depth and structure of the material are unclear.
- "Everything about YouTube" is a wide promise that is hard to judge from the outside.
- Non-Turkish speakers will not be able to follow along.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Turkish-speaking beginners and early-stage creators who want a free place to ask YouTube questions in their own language, and who would rather learn alongside a local peer group than translate English tutorials.

## Who Should Skip It

Established creators looking for advanced strategy, since nothing in the listing points to that level. Anyone who doesn't speak Turkish, and anyone who wants a clearly defined course outline before joining, may find the brief description too thin to go on.
