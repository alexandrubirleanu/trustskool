When you join, check whether the lessons cover registration basics and compliance, not just writing speed. AI can draft a proposal fast, but winning depends on following requirements exactly, and a good community will say so.

Government contracting rewards precision, so judge the community on accuracy. Look for lessons that explain how to read requirements, check eligibility and track deadlines before they get to writing. Ask whether examples of AI prompts are paired with human review steps. Members who share lessons from lost bids are a good sign, since losses teach more than wins. With 2,622 members, there should be a mix of newcomers and people with real bids behind them.
