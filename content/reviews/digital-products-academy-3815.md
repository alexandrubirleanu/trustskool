# Digital Products Academy Review 2026: Free Beginner Course, Real Value?

Digital Products Academy has attracted 9,079 members with a beginner-focused pitch: a free community "helping beginners launch simple, profitable digital product businesses without tech overwhelm," and it says a full beginners course is included inside.

## What's Actually Inside

The phrase "without tech overwhelm" is the community's stated hook, suggesting it's aimed squarely at people who find the technical side of building a digital product business, setting up sales pages, payment processing, hosting, or delivery, intimidating rather than people who already have some technical background. A full beginners course being mentioned directly is a specific, checkable claim (as opposed to vague community access), which is a slightly stronger signal than many free listings offer. That said, "#1 FREE community" is also part of the pitch, and that ranking claim isn't something that can be verified independently from the description alone, so it's worth treating as marketing language rather than an established fact.

## Who Runs It

Courtney Milam is listed as the community's founder on Skool. No further independent background, sales figures, or product-launch history for her are available in the data reviewed here, so this review limits itself to naming her as the listed owner.

## Pros

- Free to join, with a stated full beginners course included, giving newcomers a defined starting point rather than just open chat.
- Specifically targets beginners and the tech-overwhelm problem, a real barrier many people cite when trying to launch a digital product.
- A large membership of over 9,000, suggesting meaningful interest and peer activity around the topic.

## Cons

- The "#1" claim in the community's own description is unverified marketing language, not an independently confirmed ranking.
- "Profitable digital product businesses" is the stated goal, but no specific income outcomes or case studies are cited to back that framing.
- No independent reviews of this specific community's course quality were found in the data available here.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Complete beginners who want to launch a digital product but feel intimidated by the technical setup, and who are looking for a free, structured starting point rather than piecing together information themselves.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone with existing digital product or e-commerce experience looking for advanced tactics, rather than fundamentals, will likely find the content too basic. People who want documented income results before joining a "make money" adjacent community should ask for specifics, since none are published in the public listing.
