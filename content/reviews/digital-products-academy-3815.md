# Digital Products Academy Review 2026: Free Community for Beginner Digital Product Creators

## What's Actually Inside

Two things are named specifically here: the product type (digital products, likely templates, guides, or downloadable resources rather than physical goods) and the target problem (tech overwhelm), which is a real, common barrier for non-technical people trying to sell anything online. The phrase "full beginners course inside" is a concrete, checkable claim, there's a stated deliverable, not just discussion access. What's missing is detail on what kind of digital products are covered (ebooks, templates, printables, software) and which platforms members are taught to sell on.

## Pros

- Free to join, with a full beginners course included as a stated feature rather than an upsell teaser.
- Clearly targets a specific pain point (tech overwhelm) rather than generic "make money online" messaging.
- Named founder (Courtney Milam) gives something concrete to research before joining.
- Large membership base suggests an active peer group of other beginners at a similar stage.

## Cons

"#1 FREE community" is a self-declared claim with no independent ranking or source behind it in the listing. The description also doesn't specify which types of digital products are taught or which selling platforms are used, so it's unclear how the course maps to actual execution, and there's no mention of how much of the group's activity is Courtney Milam teaching directly versus members supporting each other. We found no independent reviews of this community beyond its own Skool listing.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Complete beginners with no existing tech skills who want a free, structured starting point for launching a simple digital product, especially people who've felt intimidated by more technical online business advice elsewhere.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who already has digital product experience will likely find "full beginners course" content too basic. People wanting a specific niche (ebooks only, or software only) should also confirm the actual course content matches their interest, since the description stays general on product type.
