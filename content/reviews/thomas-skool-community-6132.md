## Who It's a Good Fit For

Ecommerce store owners, dropshippers, freelance ad creators and small agencies who produce product ads regularly and want to speed that up with AI. It suits people who already understand the basics of running ads and mainly need better, faster creative production.

Anyone not selling physical or ecommerce products will find the focus narrow. People expecting a full course on media buying, targeting or store building should note that the description is about creating ad assets, not running the campaigns themselves. And those who prefer to test a community for free first may want to look at no-cost alternatives before committing to a monthly fee.
