# STEM & Screen-Free Hub Review 2026: Hands-On Projects for Families

For parents trying to cut down screen time without losing the learning value of tech-adjacent activities, STEM & Screen-Free Hub offers a specific alternative. Run by Katie Miller, this free Skool community has grown to 8,489 members around hands-on STEM projects that don't require a screen to enjoy.

## What You'll Find Inside

The description names real, concrete activity types: STEM projects generally, 3D printing ideas specifically, and "simple hands-on challenges" aimed at helping kids learn through play rather than passive consumption. That's a clearer picture than most family or parenting communities give, since it points to actual project categories rather than just "fun activities for kids." What's missing is age-range guidance, how often new projects are posted, and whether 3D printing content assumes members already own a printer.

## Strengths

- Free to join, so families can browse the project ideas before committing any time or money.
- Concrete activity categories (STEM projects, 3D printing, hands-on challenges) instead of vague parenting motivation.
- Screen-free framing directly addresses a specific, common parent concern rather than ignoring it.

## Limitations

The listing doesn't specify target age ranges, so parents of toddlers versus tweens may find the fit very different. It's also unclear whether the 3D printing content requires owning a printer already, which could be a real barrier for some families. There's no mention of how frequently new projects or challenges are posted, or whether Katie Miller is personally active in the group day to day versus content being largely self-serve. We found no independent reviews of this community beyond its own Skool listing.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Parents and educators looking for concrete, screen-free STEM activity ideas, particularly anyone interested in introducing kids to 3D printing or hands-on challenges in a low-pressure, free community setting.

## Who Should Skip It

Families without any interest in STEM or maker-style activities, or households without access to basic craft or printing materials, may find less practical value here. Parents seeking a strict, age-graded curriculum rather than a general idea hub should also set expectations accordingly, since the listing reads more like an activity idea bank than a formal program.

Given the free price tag and the specific, screen-free framing, this is a low-risk community to try if the described activity mix matches what your household is actually looking for.
