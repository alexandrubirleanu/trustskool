# STEM & Screen-Free Hub Review 2026: Hands-On STEM Projects for Families

It's a free, English-language Skool group with about 8,489 members, described as "a creative screen-free hub for families with fun STEM projects, 3D printing ideas, and simple hands-on challenges to help kids learn and play."

## What's Actually Inside

Three specific threads run through the description: STEM projects, 3D printing ideas, and simple hands-on challenges. Together they point to a resource library of physical, tactile activities parents can do with kids, science and engineering concepts taught through building and making rather than apps or video content. The 3D printing angle in particular suggests project ideas that go beyond typical "STEM kit" content into something more technical, though the listing doesn't specify whether members need their own 3D printer or whether printing is optional alongside other non-printing projects.
