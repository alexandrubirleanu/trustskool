# IDALL AI Review: AI Filmmaking for $9 a Month

## What's Actually Inside

From the listing, the focus is AI filmmaking for creators. The topics named are storytelling, trailers, ads and shareable visuals. The weekly lesson cadence is stated by the community, so a good check after joining is whether the classroom actually shows regular new uploads.

The description does not name the AI tools taught, the length of lessons or whether there are live critiques, so we stay general.

At $9 per month, the price sits at the low end of paid Skool communities. That makes it easy to try for a month and cancel if the lessons do not match your goals. Judge it on whether you finish a short film or ad that you could not have made before.

Before paying even $9, look at any free preview the owner posts publicly. Check the quality of the sample clips: consistent characters, believable motion and clean editing are hard in AI video, and the lessons should show how the work was achieved, not only the result. After joining, compare the first few weekly lessons with your goal. If you want trailers, are trailers covered in depth? If you want ads, do the examples resemble work a brand would use? Cancel quickly if the answers are no.
