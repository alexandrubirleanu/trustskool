## Cons

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Homeowners and renters planning a painting project who want trustworthy guidance on products, prep and technique before committing time and money. It suits DIYers who prefer asking a human expert to piecing together conflicting advice from the internet.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who only needs a quick tip, since free videos and store advice may suffice. People wanting to start a painting business may need training beyond home-project advice, which the description does not claim to cover. Those who dislike subscriptions should note it is a recurring monthly charge.
