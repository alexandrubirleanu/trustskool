# Ethical Publishers Review 2026: Is This Free Italian Amazon Publishing Group Legit?

Ethical Publishers is an Italian-language community about publishing books on Amazon. It has 2,090 members and, according to both the listing and its own description, it costs nothing to join.

The description makes a bold opening claim: more than €1.5M in royalties generated on Amazon, from publishers active since 2018. That figure comes from the community's own description and has not been verified by TrustSkool. It is best read as marketing, not as a typical result.

## What's Actually Inside

Translated from Italian, the description lists three concrete components, all described as free:

- **Weekly live sessions.**
- **A complete video course.**
- **Dedicated consultants.**

That is more specific than many free communities, which often promise "value" without saying what form it takes. A full video course plus recurring lives suggests a structured path for learning Amazon publishing, likely covering topics like choosing niches, producing books, and publishing through Amazon's platform. The description does not list the course modules, so the exact scope is not confirmed.

The "dedicated consultants" piece is the most unusual part of a free offer. The listing does not explain what that means in practice: one-to-one calls, chat support, or a sales conversation about a paid service. It is worth asking before assuming it is free personal coaching.

The name "Ethical Publishers" hints at a stance, perhaps against low-quality or copied content, but the description does not spell out what "ethical" means here.

No price is listed, and the description says everything is "tutto gratuito" (all free). The listing does show an affiliate program, which means members may be able to earn a commission for referrals. When free communities offer consultants, there is often a paid program somewhere downstream, though the data does not confirm one.
