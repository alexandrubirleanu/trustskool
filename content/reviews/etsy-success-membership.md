# Etsy Success Membership Review 2026: Is $27 a Month Worth It for Etsy Sellers?

Etsy Success Membership positions itself as the antidote to that, with a tagline of "Stop guessing. Start growing." It has 2,178 members on Skool and is a paid community.

## Pricing

The listing shows a price of $27 USD per month. The description matches this and adds that there is a 7 day free trial. That consistency between the price field and the description is a good sign, since it means you know what you are signing up for. As with any trial, it is worth noting the date it ends so you can decide before the first charge.

## What's Actually Inside

The description lists three components:

- **Weekly live coaching.** A recurring live session gives members a chance to ask questions and get feedback in real time.
- **Trend drops.** This suggests regular updates on what is selling or trending on Etsy, which is useful for product research.
- **Daily support.** Members are promised day to day help, likely through posts and comments in the community.

The listing does not describe a structured course or classroom, so it is unclear whether there are recorded lessons for beginners or whether the value is mostly in the live and ongoing support. It also does not say which kinds of Etsy shops the coaching suits best, such as digital downloads, print on demand, or handmade goods.

## Who Runs It

Jodi Johnson is listed as the owner. The description says the support comes from "a full time Etsy seller," but no further background is available in the data. This review does not assume anything about the size of their shop, their sales figures, or how long they have been selling.

## Pros

- Clear, consistent pricing at $27 per month with a 7 day free trial.
- Weekly live coaching is a concrete, recurring benefit.
- Trend drops address one of the hardest parts of selling on Etsy: knowing what to make next.
- A focused niche, so discussions should stay relevant to Etsy rather than drifting into general ecommerce.

## Cons

- No detail on whether there is a beginner course or only ongoing coaching.
- The type of Etsy shop the advice is geared toward is not specified.
- A monthly fee adds up over a year, especially for sellers whose shops are not yet profitable.
- The owner's experience is described only in the listing's own words.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Etsy sellers who already have a shop, or are about to open one, and want regular accountability and fresh product ideas. The live coaching format especially suits people who learn by asking questions and getting direct answers. If you want a steady rhythm of support instead of a one time course, this model fits that.

## Who Should Skip It

Sellers on a tight budget who are not yet making sales may want to use free resources first. Anyone who wants a self paced course they can finish and own should look for something with a clearly described curriculum. And if you sell mainly on Amazon, Shopify, or your own site, the Etsy specific focus will limit its usefulness.
