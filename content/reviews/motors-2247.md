# Digital Freedom Academy Review 2026: Paid Digital Marketing Skool at $27/Month

Digital Freedom Academy asks $27 a month to join, and almost 4,000 members have signed up. The pitch on its Skool page is a single line: learn the simple step-by-step process to make $1K/Day with digital marketing. That is the community's own claim, and nothing in the listing shows how many members reach that figure, so treat it as marketing copy rather than an expected outcome.

## What's Actually Inside

The description promises one thing: a step-by-step process for digital marketing aimed at a daily income target. It does not say which channel the process covers (ads, social media, affiliate offers, agency work or something else), whether it is delivered as video lessons, live calls or written guides, or how long the material takes to work through. Anyone considering the $27 monthly fee should expect to learn the real format only after joining, since the listing leaves it open. We have not invented a curriculum here, and you should be wary of any review that does.

## Who Runs It

Bj McMullan is listed as the owner. The data reviewed contains no further background, credentials or track record for him, so this review makes no claims about them.

## Pros

- The monthly price of $27 is modest next to many income-focused programs, which keeps the cost of trying it low.
- A membership of roughly 4,000 suggests a reasonably active pool of peers, though activity levels are not stated in the data.
- The promise is concrete and easy to judge: you will know quickly whether the process feels actionable.

## Cons

- The $1K/Day headline is an income claim with no supporting evidence in the listing. Results in digital marketing vary widely and are never guaranteed.
- The listing does not name the marketing method, the lesson format or the level of support, so you are paying before you can see the structure.
- A recurring subscription means the total cost grows each month you stay, so decide in advance how long you will give it.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Beginners who want a low-cost, guided starting point in digital marketing and are comfortable testing a $27 monthly subscription to see whether the teaching style suits them. It fits people who prefer a defined process over assembling advice from scattered free sources.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who needs to know the exact skills and tools taught before paying should look elsewhere or ask the owner questions first. Readers hoping for a dependable daily income figure should also skip it, because no listing can promise that. If you already run profitable campaigns, a beginner-style process may add little.
