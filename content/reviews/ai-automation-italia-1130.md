# AI Automation Italia Review 2026: Free Italian-Language Community for Business AI

AI Automation Italia is a free Skool community of roughly 3,800 members, written entirely in Italian and aimed at entrepreneurs and managers. The listing language is Italian, so this review works from a translation of its short description.

## What's Actually Inside

Translated, the description says it is the number one community in Italy for entrepreneurs and managers who want to implement AI in their company in a concrete and sustainable way. The ranking is the community's own claim and we could not verify it. The wording points to a business-application angle: putting AI to work inside an existing company, rather than learning AI as a hobby or a technical specialty. Beyond that sentence, the listing does not say which tools are covered, whether there are courses, live calls or templates, or how often anything is published. Anyone joining should expect to find out the format only from inside.

A practical way to judge a community like this is to look at what the first week inside would involve. Ask whether the posts are written by business owners describing real projects, or mostly by the owner announcing material. Check whether managers in your sector are present. The listing gives no sign either way, and with a one sentence description it is better to treat the first few weeks as a trial of fit than as a promise of results. Because membership is free, that trial costs nothing but attention.

## Who Runs It

Gianmarco Pompizii is listed as the owner of the community on Skool. The data reviewed contains nothing else about his background, so this page makes no claims about his experience or credentials.

## Pros

- Free to join, so trying it costs nothing but time.
- A clear audience: business owners and managers, not developers or students.
- Italian-language, which matters for people who want AI discussed in their own market and language.
- A membership of about 3,800 suggests there are other business owners to compare notes with.

## Cons

- The description is a single sentence, so the actual curriculum, tools and formats are unknown until you join.
- The claim of being the top community in Italy is not backed by anything in the listing.
- Non-Italian speakers will find nothing usable here, since both the listing and the community are in Italian.
- No independent reviews or outcome data were available to judge quality.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Italian-speaking owners and managers who want a free place to see how other businesses are approaching AI adoption, and who are happy to explore before committing to any particular method.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who does not read Italian should skip it. So should people looking for a documented, step by step course with named tools, since the listing does not promise one. Developers wanting deep technical material may also find the business focus too high level.
