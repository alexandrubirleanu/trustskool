# The MindBender Method™-Sales Review 2026: Do the Covert Influence Claims Hold Up?

Few community descriptions open with a headcount of winners, but this one does. According to its listing, "34 closers now earn 6 figures from this exact process." It then invites readers to "steal" The MindBender Method™ and "master covert influence."

That is a bold pitch. The community is free and lists 2,243 members, so it is easy to look inside. The question is how much of the marketing to take at face value.

## What's Actually Inside

The core offer is a named sales process, The MindBender Method™, aimed at closers. The description frames it as a method for influence and persuasion, specifically "covert" influence, which implies techniques meant to work without the buyer noticing them.

The claim that 34 closers earn six figures from this process is presented as fact in the listing, but it is the community's own statement. There is no data here showing who those closers are, over what period, or how many members did not see similar results. Precise-sounding numbers are a common marketing device, so treat this one as unverified.

Beyond the method's name and its persuasion angle, the description does not say how the material is taught: course modules, call breakdowns, scripts, or coaching.

## Who Runs It

Joon Choi is listed as the owner. No further background is available in the data, so this review does not speculate about his sales history or how the method was developed.

## Pros

- Free, so you can evaluate the method yourself rather than paying to find out.
- Specific focus on closing technique instead of general business advice.
- A named, branded system suggests a defined framework rather than scattered tips.

## Cons

- "34 closers now earn 6 figures" is an unverified results claim.
- Marketing built around "covert" influence may not sit well with people who prefer transparent, consent-based selling.
- No details on format, depth, or support.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Working closers or aspiring salespeople who are curious about persuasion frameworks and want to study one more method for free. People who already sell for a living and can judge techniques critically will get the most from it.

## Who Should Skip It

If manipulation-flavored sales language makes you uneasy, the positioning alone may be a reason to pass. Beginners hoping for a guaranteed path to six figures should be skeptical of any community that leads with income claims, including this one.
