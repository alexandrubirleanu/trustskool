# Zenith Forex Review 2026: Questions to Ask About a Free Forex Automation Community

Few phrases in trading deserve more scrutiny than "hands-free." Zenith Forex uses it in its pitch: "World-class Hands-Free Beginner Friendly Trading Automation. Precision. Discipline." The listing adds a location, Menlyn Maine in Pretoria, South Africa. It is a free English-language Skool community with 2,968 members.

## What's Actually Inside

The description points to automated trading, meaning software, such as bots or expert advisors, that places forex trades according to rules. It stresses "beginner friendly" and "hands-free," which together suggest members are meant to use ready-made automation rather than learn to trade manually. It also uses words such as "precision" and "discipline," which describe an ideal, not a result.

There is a lot the listing does not say. It does not describe the strategy, the software, the markets, or how the automation is supplied. It gives no performance data, no explanation of fees, and no information about brokers. Those omissions matter, because in automated forex the questions that decide whether something is safe are exactly these: who holds your money, which broker is used, and how performance is reported and audited.

Forex is a leveraged market in which many retail traders lose money, and automation does not remove that risk. A bot can lose just as quickly as a person, sometimes faster. "World-class" is a self-assessment with no supporting evidence in the data reviewed here. Be especially wary of any arrangement where you are asked to deposit with a particular broker, hand over account access, or pay for a paid tier on the strength of screenshots.

Also verify whether the people behind it are authorised to give financial services where you live. A stated address is not a licence, and the listing offers no regulatory information.

With 2,968 members, the group is moderate in size, though size says nothing about outcomes.
