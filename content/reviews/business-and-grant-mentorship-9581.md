# Business & Grant Community Review 2026: Free Skool Community for Funding, Grants & Credit

Ready to gain access to business funding, grants, and a stronger personal credit score, all without paying to join? That's the direct pitch behind Business & Grant Community, a free Skool group that has grown to 7,135 members around exactly those three pillars.

## What's Actually Inside

The description names three distinct areas rather than a single vague promise: business funding, grants, and personal credit. That combination suggests members get exposure to more than one funding path, direct capital access, grant opportunities that don't need to be repaid, and credit-building tactics that can improve loan and lending terms down the line. The phrase "& more" leaves room for additional resources not spelled out in the listing, but the core three topics give a reasonably clear sense of what a new member should expect to find: information and community discussion aimed at entrepreneurs trying to fund a business through multiple channels at once, rather than one narrow tactic.

Jekwenta Primm is listed as the owner of this community on Skool.

## Pros

- Covers three separate funding angles, business funding, grants, and personal credit, instead of pushing a single narrow method.
- Free to join, which matters for a niche where paid "funding secrets" courses are common.
- A membership base of over 7,100 suggests an active, established group rather than a brand-new listing.
- Grant access, if genuinely covered in depth, is a meaningfully different resource than most credit-focused communities offer, since grants don't need to be repaid.

## Cons

- The description doesn't explain how grants are sourced or vetted, so it's unclear whether members get curated grant leads or just general education on finding them.
- No specific credit score targets, funding amounts, or success metrics are given here, unlike some funding-focused competitors that name concrete numbers.
- No independent reviews were available to confirm how active the community discussion actually is beyond the stated member count.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Entrepreneurs and small business owners who want a free entry point into business funding, grant opportunities, and credit-building strategy, especially those who haven't yet explored non-loan funding options like grants and want a starting community to learn the landscape.

## Who Should Skip It

Business owners who need highly specific, numbers-driven guidance, such as an exact credit score target or a guaranteed funding amount, may find this listing too broad, since it doesn't quantify outcomes the way some competing communities do. Anyone who has already secured funding and credit and needs more advanced scaling advice will also likely need a more specialized group than this one.
