# Ecom Da Zero Review 2026: A Free Italian Starting Point for Shopify and Amazon Sellers?

"Da zero" means "from zero" in Italian, and that captures the whole idea of this community. Ecom Da Zero is an Italian-language Skool group for people who want to launch an online store without prior experience, using either Shopify or Amazon. It is free and lists 2,410 members.

## What's Actually Inside

Translated, the description calls it the definitive community for launching your e-commerce business from scratch with Shopify or Amazon, offering updated strategies for getting results with online sales.

Three details are worth pulling out:

- **Two platforms.** Shopify (your own store) and Amazon (selling on a marketplace) are quite different businesses. Covering both gives members options, though it may also mean each gets less depth.
- **Beginner focus.** "From zero" signals that the material is aimed at people starting out.
- **Updated strategies.** The emphasis on current tactics makes sense in e-commerce, where advertising costs and platform rules change often.

"Definitive community" is the group's own description of itself, not something the data can verify. The listing also does not say whether content comes as courses, live sessions, or discussion threads, and it does not share any sales figures or member results.

## Pros

- Free, which lowers the barrier for beginners still testing whether e-commerce suits them.
- Covers both Shopify and Amazon, letting newcomers compare models.
- Italian-language content for sellers who prefer to learn in their native language.

## Cons

- "Definitive" is a marketing claim with nothing in the data to support it.
- No information about format, depth, or how often strategies are updated.
- Results "with online sales" are promised in general terms only.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Italian speakers who are curious about starting an online store and have not yet chosen between Shopify and Amazon. Complete beginners who want a free community to learn the basics and ask questions should feel at home.

## Who Should Skip It

Established sellers with steady revenue may find a from-scratch community too elementary. Anyone who does not read Italian, or who sells on platforms other than Shopify and Amazon, will get limited value.
