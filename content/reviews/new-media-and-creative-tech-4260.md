# Creative Leads (Pro) Review: Jobs and Collabs for Designers at $9/Month

Creative Leads (Pro) is one of the more concrete offers in the data. It is an indie community focused on jobs, collaborations and freelancing in design, art and creative technology, priced at $9 per month. It has 2,684 members, and that number climbed from 2,643 to 2,684 across the three dates we track.

## What's Actually Inside

The listing says it is an indie community for jobs, collabs and freelancing in the design, art and creative tech space. It adds one distinctive promise: all leads are sourced beyond LinkedIn. In other words, the opportunities shared are meant to come from outside the platform where most designers already look, which is the main selling point.

What the listing does not say is how often leads are posted, what kind of roles they are (full-time, contract, one-off gigs), which regions they cover or whether there is vetting. Those are the questions to ask yourself when you first log in.

The "Pro" in the name suggests a paid tier alongside a free version, but the data does not describe a free offer, so we only report the $9 monthly price shown.

## Who Runs It

The owner is listed as Ameen Insan. The activity data we hold shows the owner active on 28 of the last 30 days and with 173 contributions, which points to a hands-on presence. We hold no further biography and will not add one.

## Pros

- Clear purpose: leads, collaborations and freelance work in creative fields.
- Leads said to come from sources beyond LinkedIn, so less crowded.
- A low $9 monthly cost.
- Owner activity looks consistent in the data.

## Cons

- Lead quality and volume cannot be checked from the listing.
- Creative job markets vary by country, and the region focus is not stated.
- A subscription can feel wasted in months with few relevant leads.
- No stated support such as portfolio feedback or contract advice.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Freelance designers, illustrators, motion artists and creative technologists who want a steady stream of opportunities without paying much, and who are ready to reply to leads quickly.

## Who Should Skip It

If you want training, a portfolio review or mentoring, this is a lead board, not a school. Senior creatives with a full pipeline may find little they do not already see. Check a month of postings before you decide to keep paying.
