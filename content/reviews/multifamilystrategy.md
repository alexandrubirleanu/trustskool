# Multifamily Strategy Community Review 2026: $1/Month Real Estate Deal Network

One dollar a month is the listed price of the Multifamily Strategy Community, a Skool group with about 3,250 members. Its description is a four-verb list: "Find, Fund, Discuss, & Network on Multifamily Deals."

## What's Actually Inside

Each verb points to a stage of multifamily investing. Finding refers to locating deals, funding to raising or arranging capital, discussing to exchanging views, and networking to meeting other investors. Multifamily means residential properties with several units, such as apartment buildings. The listing gives only the four verbs. It does not say whether there is a deal board, lender introductions, training, calls or a member directory.

The $1 monthly price is unusual. It may serve as a low barrier to filter for people who are serious enough to pay anything, but the data does not say why it is set this way. It would be reasonable to ask whether the community offers any higher-priced products, though the listing does not mention any. It is also worth asking how the owner earns from the community at that price, since the data is silent on it. A newcomer should read posts carefully and treat any deal shared by a stranger as something to verify independently.

## Who Runs It

The listing names Christian Osgood as the owner of this community. That is the only information about the person included in the data behind this review, so no background, credentials or track record are described here.

## Pros

- A nominal price of $1 a month makes it essentially risk free to try.
- A specific property type, multifamily, with a clear four-part purpose.
- Networking and funding are included in the scope, not just education.
- About 3,250 members offers a useful pool of potential contacts.

## Cons

- The listing does not say how deals or funding are actually shared.
- No details about the quality or vetting of deals posted.
- No education component is described, so beginners may lack guidance.
- Nothing in the data confirms completed deals or member outcomes.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Investors and aspiring investors who want a low-cost place to meet others interested in multifamily property and to compare deal ideas.

## Who Should Skip It

Complete beginners who want a structured course will not find one described. Anyone expecting a source of vetted deals or guaranteed funding should skip it, and should do independent due diligence on any opportunity.
