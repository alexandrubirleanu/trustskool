# SHINSHI NETWORK Review 2026: Free Men's Support and Personal Change Programme

SHINSHI NETWORK is a community for men looking for advice and support, with about 3,620 members. The platform lists it as free, and the page uses strong promotional language.

## What's Actually Inside

The description asks whether men need advice or support, or want a transformational shift, and then says a 30 Day Programme guarantees abundance, love, purpose and freedom. Two things stand out. The programme is the one concrete element: it runs 30 days. And the guarantee is very broad. Outcomes such as love and abundance cannot be assured by any program, and the listing explains no refund terms or criteria, so we treat the guarantee as marketing and not a verifiable promise. The listing does not describe daily tasks, coaching or group sessions.

When a listing uses the word guarantee, it is reasonable to ask what exactly is guaranteed and what happens if it does not occur. Here the answer is not given. The outcomes named are abundance, love, purpose and freedom, which are personal and cannot be measured in a standard way. The thirty day programme itself may well be a useful structure, but members should treat it as a prompt for self reflection and not as a contract. Reading the join page for terms is advisable.

## Who Runs It

Craig Ing is listed as the owner. The data has no credentials or background for him, so none are stated, and the listing does not say whether he is a trained counsellor.

## Pros

- Free to join.
- A defined 30 day programme gives a clear time frame.
- Peer support for men may be valuable to people who feel isolated.
- Around 3,620 members.

## Cons

- The guarantee of abundance, love, purpose and freedom is unverifiable and very broad.
- Programme content and structure are not described.
- No qualifications are listed, which matters for advice on personal struggles.
- Not a replacement for professional mental health support.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Men who want a free peer community and a time boxed programme to push personal change, and who are skeptical about big promises.

## Who Should Skip It

Skip it if you are dealing with serious mental health issues and need qualified care. Also skip it if you dislike promotional language or want evidence of outcomes before joining.
