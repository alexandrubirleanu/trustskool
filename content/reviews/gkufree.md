# GKU (Free) Review 2026: Free Skool Community for Goalkeepers

Most sports communities on Skool try to serve an entire sport at once. GKU (Free) doesn't, its description is a single sentence: "The free community for goalkeepers who want to get better." Almost 9,000 members have joined a community built around one position in one sport.

## What's Actually Inside

By focusing narrowly on goalkeepers rather than soccer players generally, the community implicitly signals position-specific content: shot-stopping, positioning, distribution, or footwork, areas that generic team-training resources tend to skip over. That kind of niche focus is easier to evaluate than a broad "improve at soccer" pitch, since it's clear who the content is written for. The "Free" label in the name also suggests there may be a paid counterpart, which prospective members should look into separately if they want to know what's held back for a paid tier.

## Who Runs It

Andreas Vaikla is listed as the owner of this community on Skool. No further independent background or credentials are available in the data reviewed here, so this review sticks to that basic attribution.

## Pros

- Free membership, which matters in a sport where specialized goalkeeper coaching often carries a real price tag.
- Narrow, position-specific focus rather than generic team-sport advice.
- Nearly 9,000 members suggests a genuinely active goalkeeper-only community to compare notes and progress with.
- The owner shows meaningful activity history on Skool (hundreds of contributions), a signal of ongoing engagement rather than an abandoned listing.

## Cons

- The description doesn't specify age group, skill level, or whether content targets youth, amateur, or more advanced keepers.
- No specific training methodology, drills, or curriculum is named in the listing itself.
- No independent reviews were found beyond the community's own Skool page.
- It's unclear whether coaching comes from currently active professional goalkeepers or from generally experienced members, a distinction worth checking before joining for serious development purposes.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Goalkeepers at any level who want free, position-specific training content and a community of other keepers, rather than general soccer coaching aimed at outfield players. Parents of young goalkeepers looking for a starting point may also find it a reasonable, no-cost way to explore the position.

## Who Should Skip It

Outfield players, coaches looking for whole-team training resources, or goalkeepers who need a specific age-group or competitive-level program not described here. Elite keepers already working with a personal position coach may also find a free general community less useful than one-on-one training.
