# AirBnB Mastery Review: A German Airbnb Business Community with Beginner and Platinum Tracks

Short-term rentals attract plenty of ambitious beginners, and AirBnB Mastery speaks to them in German. The community has 2,521 members and tells you its structure upfront: start with the beginner Airbnb course or grow with a Platinum membership on the way to a profitable Airbnb business.

## What's Actually Inside

The description, translated, offers two paths. The first is a beginner Airbnb course for newcomers. The second is a Platinum membership for those who want to go further. That suggests a tiered offer, with a free entry point and a more advanced paid level.

The data lists no price for the community itself, so we treat the main page as free. We do not know what the Platinum tier costs or what it includes, and we will not guess. The listing also does not cover whether the model is rental arbitrage, hosting your own property or managing for others, which matters a great deal in practice.

Membership has been steady at 2,520 to 2,521 across the four tracked dates, so there is no obvious growth.

Before paying for any upgrade, it helps to ask a few plain questions: what rental model is taught, in which markets, and with what documented numbers for occupancy, costs and local permits. Short-term rental rules differ by city and country, so general advice only goes so far, and a good course will say where it stops.

## Who Runs It

Marvin Opper is listed as the owner. The data does not provide his property or hosting history, so we make no claims about it. Anyone investing in short-term rentals should ask what experience backs the course, including real numbers on occupancy and costs.

## Pros

- Clear two-step structure: beginner course, then advanced membership.
- German-language, useful for the DACH market.
- Free community page based on the data.
- A topic with an established business model.

## Cons

- Platinum pricing is not shown, so costs are unclear.
- No detail about the method or legal and tax considerations.
- Local regulations on short-term rentals vary widely and can change.
- The listing makes the "profitable" promise with no figures.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

German speakers who are curious about hosting or managing short-term rentals and want an introduction before spending real money on property or paid tiers.

## Who Should Skip It

Skip it if you need market-specific legal advice, as rules differ by city. Experienced hosts will likely find the beginner content too basic. Be careful about committing to expensive upgrades before you have seen what the free material delivers.
