# Het AI Lokaal Review 2026: Free Dutch-Language Community for Learning AI and Automations

"Het AI Lokaal" translates to The AI Classroom, and the name sets the expectation: a place to learn. This Dutch-language Skool community has about 3,280 members and no listed price. Its description, translated, says: "Learn to work with AI step by step. Build automations, get feedback, grow together."

## What's Actually Inside

Three verbs structure the offer: learn, build and grow. Learning is described as step by step, which suggests a guided order and not a loose feed. Building refers to automations, though the listing does not name the tools or platforms used. Receiving feedback is mentioned as a feature, which usually means members can share what they have made and get comments, but who gives that feedback (the owner, other members or both) is not stated. "Grow together" points to a community element.

What is missing is a curriculum list, a schedule, or details on how often new material arrives. For a listing with this much practical language, those gaps are the main thing to check after joining. For a newcomer, the practical test is whether the first steps are easy to find and whether a question posted by a beginner gets a useful answer within a reasonable time. The listing makes no promise on either point.

## Who Runs It

The listing names Yasar Yigitturk as the owner of this community. That is the only information about the person included in the data behind this review, so no background, credentials or track record are described here.

## Pros

- Free to join.
- Dutch-language, a smaller market than English or Spanish where AI learning resources are scarcer.
- The feedback promise gives members a reason to share work, not only watch.
- A step-by-step framing that suits beginners.
- Roughly 3,280 members.

## Cons

- Tools and platforms are not named.
- No information about the source of the feedback or its turnaround time.
- No curriculum or schedule in the listing.
- No member results or testimonials in the data.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Dutch speakers who are new to AI at work and want a free, guided-feeling place to build simple automations while getting input from others.

## Who Should Skip It

Advanced practitioners who want deep technical material, and anyone who does not read Dutch, will probably get little from it. People who need a certified course should look elsewhere.
