# No Theory Club Review 2026: Learn Guitar Songs for $9/Month Without Music Theory

For $9/month, No Theory Club promises something narrower than most guitar communities: learning to play your favorite songs on guitar without first working through music theory. It's a specific pitch, and with 8,725 members it has clearly found an audience for that approach.

Plenty of guitar education online starts with scales, key signatures, and chord construction before ever getting to a full song. No Theory Club's description positions itself as the opposite of that path, aiming songs first and theory as optional or skipped entirely.

## What's Actually Inside

The description is short but pointed. Rather than teaching scales, chord theory, or ear training from the ground up, the stated focus is song-based learning, presumably tabs, chord charts, or play-along lessons built around recognizable songs. For $9 a month, that's a low-commitment way to test whether a theory-light, repertoire-first approach actually works for a given player's learning style.

## Pros

- A clear, specific teaching philosophy, songs first, theory optional, rather than a generic "learn guitar" pitch.
- Affordable at $9/month compared to many paid coaching communities.
- A large enough membership to suggest an active community around new lessons.

## Cons

- Players who eventually want deeper music theory may find this approach limiting long-term.
- The description doesn't list which songs, genres, or skill levels are covered.
- No independent reviews were available here beyond the Skool listing itself.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Beginner to intermediate guitarists who want to play recognizable songs quickly without getting bogged down in theory, and who value momentum and motivation over a slower, foundational curriculum.

## Who Should Skip It

Guitarists who specifically want to build strong music theory fundamentals, or advanced players past the song-covers stage who are looking for technique-focused or improvisation-focused instruction instead.
