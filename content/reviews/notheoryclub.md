# No Theory Club Review 2026: Learn Guitar Songs Without Music Theory

No Theory Club has built a community of over 8,700 guitar learners around a specific promise stated right in its name and description: learn your favorite songs on guitar without needing to study music theory first. It's priced at $9 a month, a low enough price point that it reads more like an accessible add-on than a premium coaching program.

## What's Actually Inside

The "without music theory" angle is the whole pitch here, and it's a real methodology choice, not just marketing language. Plenty of guitar learners stall out trying to memorize scales and chord theory before they ever play a recognizable song, so a song-first approach that skips straight to playing favorite tracks addresses a genuine pain point for hobbyist players. What the description doesn't clarify is which songs, genres, or skill levels are covered, or whether lessons are structured as a progression from beginner to intermediate or just a growing library of song tutorials. That's a meaningful gap for anyone trying to judge fit before paying. The $9 price also functions as a low commitment test: it's cheap enough that a curious guitarist could try a month without much financial risk, which matters for anyone unsure whether the song-first method will actually click for their own learning style.
