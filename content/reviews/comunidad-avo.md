# Comunidad de Ventas Online Review 2026: Free Spanish Sales Community?

Comunidad de Ventas Online, "Online Sales Community," is a free, Spanish-language Skool group with 6,835 members built around a specific career path: becoming a digital setter or closer, roles common in high-ticket sales for online businesses, and generating income remotely.

## What's Actually Inside

The description is short but specific: "Conviertete en Setter o Closer Digital y genera ingresos desde cualquier lugar," become a digital setter or closer and generate income from anywhere. Setters and closers are two distinct sales roles in the high-ticket online sales world, setters qualify leads and book calls, closers handle the actual sales conversation, so the community appears oriented toward training or connecting people into these specific remote sales positions rather than general entrepreneurship or marketing.

## Who Runs It

Paula Barreto is listed as the community's owner on Skool. No further public biography or professional history is available in the data reviewed here, so this review sticks to that basic attribution.

## Pros

- Free to join, notable given that setter and closer training is frequently sold as a paid program elsewhere.
- A specific, named career path (setter/closer) rather than vague "make money online" framing, which should make the content more directly actionable.
- A large membership base, nearly 6,835 members, suggests real ongoing interest in remote sales roles among Spanish speakers.

## Cons

- The description doesn't explain how members actually land setter or closer roles, whether through direct placement, referrals, or purely self-study, so the path from community to paid work is unclear.
- No independent reviews or outcome data were found to confirm how many members actually secure paying setter or closer positions.
- As a Spanish-language community, it's limited to Spanish-speaking members, which narrows its reach.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Spanish-speaking individuals interested specifically in breaking into digital setting or closing as a remote income path, and who are comfortable in a free, community-driven learning environment where results likely depend heavily on individual effort and follow-through rather than a guaranteed placement.

## Who Should Skip It

Non-Spanish speakers, and anyone looking for a broader digital marketing or business-building education rather than sales-role-specific training, will likely find this too narrow for their goals. People expecting a guaranteed job placement rather than training and community support should also temper expectations accordingly.
