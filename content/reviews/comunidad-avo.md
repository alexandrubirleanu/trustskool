# Comunidad de Ventas Online Review 2026: Setter and Closer Training in Spanish

What does it actually take to earn money as a remote sales rep for someone else's business? Comunidad de Ventas Online, a free Spanish-language Skool community with 6,835 members, frames its whole pitch around one specific answer: become a "setter" or "closer" and generate income from anywhere.

## What's Actually Inside

The terms setter and closer come from the high-ticket sales world. A setter typically qualifies leads and books calls, while a closer runs the actual sales conversation and closes the deal, both of which are commission-based, remote-friendly roles that don't require building a product or business of one's own. By naming both roles specifically, the description signals that the community is teaching a sales skill set members can apply working for other companies, rather than a general "start your own business" curriculum.

"Genera ingresos desde cualquier lugar" (generate income from anywhere) is the community's location-independence promise, consistent with how setter and closer roles are usually structured as remote, contract, or commission-based positions rather than in-office jobs.

## Pros

- Names a specific, learnable skill set (setter and closer sales roles) instead of a vague "make money online" promise.
- Free to join, lowering the barrier for people testing whether remote sales work suits them.
- Location independence is a realistic feature of setter and closer work, not an overstated claim.
- A large, active-looking membership base of nearly 6,900 in a Spanish-speaking niche that's less saturated than English-language equivalents.

## Cons

- The description doesn't specify what industries or products members would be setting or closing for, which affects how transferable the skills are.
- Setter and closer income is typically commission-based, so earnings aren't guaranteed simply from joining or completing training, a nuance the listing doesn't address.
- No independent reviews of the community were found beyond its Skool listing, and the description is in Spanish only, so non-Spanish speakers will need translation to fully evaluate it.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Spanish-speaking individuals who want to learn remote, commission-based sales skills and are comfortable working as a setter or closer for other people's offers rather than building their own product.

## Who Should Skip It

People looking to build their own product or brand rather than sell for someone else, and non-Spanish speakers who would need to work through a language barrier to get real value from the community.
