# The AI Blueprint Review 2026: Free Spanish AI Automation Community

"The AI Blueprint" markets itself in Spanish as a community for people who want to master automation, and it has clearly found an audience: 9,096 members, described in its own listing as over 8,000 active members. It's run by Pau Berenguer and costs nothing to join.

## What's Actually Inside

The core topic is automation, framed with an "AI" name in the community title, so the working assumption is AI-powered automation tools and workflows rather than automation in a broader engineering sense. The listing leans heavily on growth marketing language, "100% free," "+8K active members," "you can see more", which reads as recruitment copy more than a syllabus. That's a meaningful gap: there's no mention of which automation tools are taught (no-code platforms, custom scripts, specific AI models), what skill level is assumed, or what a member actually produces by the end.

## Pros

- Free to join, with no cost barrier mentioned anywhere in the listing.
- Large, apparently active member base, which the community itself highlights as a selling point.
- Clear single-word focus on automation as the topic, situated within the broader AI space.
- Spanish-language framing serves an audience often underserved by English-first AI communities.

## Cons

The description reads more like an ad for joining than a description of the actual curriculum, listing member counts and "see more" prompts rather than specific lessons, tools, or outcomes. There's no detail on what "mastering automation" concretely involves, no named tools, no sample projects, no stated skill progression, and no indication of how the founder, Pau Berenguer, is personally involved in running the day-to-day content. We found no independent reviews of this community beyond its own Skool listing.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Spanish-speaking beginners curious about AI-driven automation who want a free, high-membership community to start exploring the topic, with the understanding that specifics will need to be discovered by joining rather than read from the listing.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone wanting a syllabus or curriculum outline before committing time should be cautious, since none is given here. Non-Spanish speakers will also hit a language barrier, and people who dislike heavy recruitment-style marketing copy in a community's own pitch may want to look elsewhere first. Members who already have working automation experience are also likely to outgrow a listing pitched primarily at newcomers exploring the topic for the first time.
