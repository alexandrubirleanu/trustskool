# The AI Blueprint Review 2026: Is This Free Spanish Automation Community Legit?

"100% GRATIS" and "+8K Miembros Activos" are the two claims The AI Blueprint leads with in its own description, a free Spanish-language Skool community pitched as a space for people who want to master automation. Our data puts the actual membership at 9,096, which is consistent with, if slightly above, that "+8K" figure.

## What's Actually Inside

The description is short and centers entirely on one word: automation, positioned with a sparkle emoji as something aspirational rather than explained in detail. It doesn't say which tools, platforms, or automation categories are covered, whether that's no-code workflow builders, AI agent setups, or business process automation more broadly. The claim of "8K+ active members" is worth noting as a self-reported figure rather than an independently audited one, though it does roughly track with the total membership count in our own data, which lends it some credibility.

## Who Runs It

Pau Berenguer is listed as the community's owner on Skool. No independent background, technical credentials, or automation track record for him are available in the data reviewed here, so this review sticks to naming the listed owner rather than inferring expertise.

## Pros

- Completely free, with no cost barrier to exploring automation content and community.
- A large, apparently active membership base, which the description itself highlights and our member count roughly corroborates.
- Focused specifically on automation, a genuinely in-demand skill area, rather than a scattershot of unrelated topics.

## Cons

- The description doesn't specify tools, platforms, or skill level, making it hard to judge depth or relevance before joining.
- Self-reported activity claims like "+8K Miembros Activos" should be treated as marketing framing rather than independently verified engagement data.
- No independent reviews of this specific community's teaching quality were found in the data available here.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Spanish-speaking beginners and intermediate learners curious about automation who want a free, low-commitment community to start exploring the topic and are comfortable discovering the specific tools and methods once inside.

## Who Should Skip It

Non-Spanish speakers will find limited value here. Anyone who already works professionally in automation and wants advanced, specialized content, rather than a broad community for people newer to the topic, may find the offering too general. People who need a documented curriculum before joining should ask directly, since the public listing doesn't lay one out.
