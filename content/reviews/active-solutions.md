# Active Solutions Review 2026: Free AI Growth Resources for Small Businesses

Active Solutions says its mission is making elite enterprise solutions accessible for small businesses. The description repeats that the community is 100% free, and the platform data agrees, with no price listed. It has about 3,630 members.

## What's Actually Inside

Two promises appear in the description: how to get more clients, and how to scale faster with AI. The framing is that large company tools and methods can be adapted for small businesses. That is a business-growth theme with an AI angle, but the listing does not say what is delivered. There is no named toolset, no lessons, no templates and no calls. Without those details, it is hard to tell whether the community is mainly discussion or contains structured training. The explicit word free is a plus, since it removes pricing ambiguity.

The enterprise to small business angle is common, and its value depends on whether the community translates big company practices into steps a small team can follow. Look for concrete examples such as outreach scripts, automations or case studies shared by members. The listing gives none, so it is unclear whether that translation happens. The word free also invites a check on whether the group points toward paid services, which is common but not stated here.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Small business owners and freelancers who want a free place to see how AI might help them find clients and grow.

## Who Should Skip It

Skip it if you need a defined course on a particular tool, or if you run a large organization, since the community is aimed at small businesses. People who have already built an AI driven sales process may find the general framing too basic.
