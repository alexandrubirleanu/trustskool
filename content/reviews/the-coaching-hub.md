# The Coaching Hub Review 2026: Does It Deliver a Real Roadmap for Growing a Coaching Business?

Plenty of coaches are good at coaching and far less comfortable with marketing, sales, and systems. The Coaching Hub targets that gap. It has 1,853 members, and the listing shows no price, so it appears to be free to join.

Its description makes a direct offer: if you want a proven roadmap, a simple system, and personalized support to grow your coaching business, join.

## What's Actually Inside

The pitch rests on three elements.

**A proven roadmap.** This implies a step by step path, probably from defining an offer to finding clients. The word "proven" comes from the description, and the listing contains no data showing results, so it should be read as a claim rather than a fact.

**A simple system.** Simplicity is a sensible selling point for coaches who feel overwhelmed by funnels, ads, and tech. What the system actually involves (content, outreach, referrals, or something else) is not specified.

**Personalized support.** This is the most interesting promise, because personalized help is hard to deliver at scale in a free community of nearly two thousand people. The listing does not explain how it works, whether through live Q&A calls, feedback on posts, or a paid upgrade.

The data also shows a 50% affiliate commission, which is high. That strongly suggests a paid offer exists somewhere in this ecosystem, and the personalized element may be part of it. The listing does not confirm this, so it is worth asking what is included for free before you rely on that support.

## Who Runs It

Nathan J Bishop is listed as the owner. No further background is available in the data, so this review does not make claims about his own coaching business or client results.

## Pros

- Appears free to join based on the listing.
- A clear audience: coaches who want to grow their business.
- Promises both structure (a roadmap) and support, not just content.
- "Simple system" framing may suit coaches who dislike complex marketing.

## Cons

- "Proven" is unverified; no outcomes are shown in the data.
- Unclear how personalized support works for free members.
- A 50% affiliate rate hints at paid upsells that the listing does not describe.
- No detail on coaching niches covered or the methods taught.
- Owner background is not available in the data.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

New and early stage coaches, in life, health, business, or other niches, who have a skill but lack a reliable way to attract clients. It suits people who want a step by step plan and a group of peers facing the same challenges, and who are happy to start free and evaluate any paid options later.

## Who Should Skip It

Established coaches with a full calendar and working systems may find a beginner roadmap repetitive. People who are not running, or planning to run, a coaching business have little reason to join. And if you are wary of communities that lead into paid programs, ask about pricing clearly before investing time.
