# EF Trading Review 2026: Free Spanish-Language Trading Community

EF Trading calls itself "la mejor Comunidad Gratuita de Trading de habla hispana," the best free trading community in the Spanish-speaking world. It is a free Skool group with 3,081 members. Superlatives like that are easy to write and hard to check, so this review looks at what the rest of the description actually says.

## What's Actually Inside

After the headline claim, the description lists three things: analysis and projections ("análisis y proyecciones"), training ("formación"), and constant support ("acompañamiento constante"). Put together, that suggests a mix of market commentary, educational material and ongoing guidance from the team or other members.

Each item raises a question the listing leaves open. Analysis and projections could mean daily chart posts, weekly outlooks or live sessions, and the markets covered are not named. Projections in particular are opinions about the future. They can be useful as a way of seeing how someone reasons about a chart, but they are not forecasts you can rely on, and markets regularly move against confident calls. "Formación" could range from a handful of videos to a full curriculum. "Acompañamiento constante" is a promise about responsiveness, and whether it holds depends on staffing and community culture, neither of which appears in the data reviewed here.

The word "gratuita" is also in the slug, which hints at a paid version somewhere, though the listing does not confirm one. If analysis is posted in the free group, ask yourself whether the paid offer, if there is one, is the real product.

On size, 3,081 members is moderate for a Spanish-language trading group. A Spanish-speaking audience spans many countries, so check that the content and any recommended brokers or platforms suit your location and local regulation.

## Who Runs It

The listed owner is Elie Farage. No further verified background, trading record or credentials are available in the data reviewed here.

## Pros

- Free membership, with analysis, training and support all named as part of the offer.
- Spanish-language focus serves a large audience that is often given less trading education than English speakers.
- A clear description of the three content types, which is more specific than many trading listings.
- A moderate community size that may allow real conversation.

## Cons

- "The best" is an unverified claim, and no independent reviews were found in the data reviewed.
- Markets, timeframes and methods are not specified.
- Projections can create false confidence when members treat them as predictions.
- The listing does not clarify whether a paid tier sits behind the free group.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Spanish-speaking beginners and intermediate learners who want to watch how analysis is presented, learn terminology in their own language, and who will test every idea on a demo account before using real money.

## Who Should Skip It

People seeking guaranteed profits or signals to follow without understanding them. Trading carries a real risk of loss, and nothing on this page is financial advice. Anyone who cannot afford to lose the money they would trade should avoid it altogether.
