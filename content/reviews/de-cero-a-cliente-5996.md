# Automatiza y Cobra. Review 2026: $10/Month Spanish AI Automation Community

Priced at $10 a month, Automatiza y Cobra. (roughly, "Automate and Get Paid") is a Spanish-language community with about 3,300 members. The owner describes it in the first person, which gives the listing an unusual tone of personal testimony rather than product pitch.

## What's Actually Inside

Translated, the description reads: "What really worked for me to make money with AI. Tools, flows and real mistakes, unfiltered. Offer your services or automate your business." Four ingredients are named: tools, workflows (the word "flujos" is typically used for automation flows), real mistakes, and two possible uses, selling services to clients or automating one's own business.

The mention of mistakes is worth noting, since many communities in this space highlight only wins. Still, the listing does not specify which tools are covered, whether lessons are video or text, or how often content is added. The community's internal URL name, which refers to going from zero to a client, hints at a client-acquisition angle, but the description itself does not elaborate on it.

## Who Runs It

The listing names Pedro Bon as the owner of this community. That is the only information about the person included in the data behind this review, so no background, credentials or track record are described here. The description speaks from personal experience with making money using AI, but the data includes no details of that experience, so none are repeated here.

## Pros

- Low price at $10 per month.
- Practical framing around tools and flows rather than abstract AI theory.
- Openly mentions mistakes, which suggests a candid approach.
- Two clear paths: sell automation services or automate your own business.
- Spanish-language material for a market with fewer AI-automation resources than English.

## Cons

- The tools and platforms are not named.
- No curriculum, format or update schedule in the listing.
- The phrase "make money with AI" is a general promise, and no income figures are given.
- No independent reviews in the data.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Spanish-speaking freelancers or small business owners who want an inexpensive way to explore AI automation, either to offer it as a service or to cut work in their own business.

## Who Should Skip It

Readers who need an in-depth technical course or a certified program will not find one described here. Those who do not read Spanish, or who want guaranteed earnings from AI, should look elsewhere.
