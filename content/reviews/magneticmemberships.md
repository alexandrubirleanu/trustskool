# Magnetic Memberships Review 2026: Recurring Revenue with an AI Workforce

Magnetic Memberships pitches a business that "runs without you," powered by memberships and an AI workforce. The English-language community has 4,018 members, sits in the money category, and lists no paid price. The three promised outcomes are recurring revenue, real leverage and "your time back."

## What's Actually Inside

The description combines two ideas. The first is the membership model, where customers pay on a recurring basis for access to content, community or services. The second is an "AI workforce," presumably AI tools or agents that handle parts of the operation. Together, the claim is that a membership business can be run with minimal owner involvement.

That is an attractive thesis, and also one that deserves scrutiny. The listing does not explain what the AI workforce consists of, which tasks it handles, or which tools are used. It does not say what kind of membership you would build, for what audience, or how members are acquired. "Runs without you" is a goal that is rarely fully achieved, especially early on, when acquiring and retaining members usually takes direct work.

There is also no mention of lessons, templates, calls or case studies. If you join, look for examples of real membership businesses built using the method, and check whether the AI components are practical or just conceptual.

An obvious point: the community seems to teach the membership model, and Skool itself is a membership platform, so some of the material may overlap with running a community of your own.

## Who Runs It

The owner is listed as Dr. Stephen Buscema. The data we have contains no information about his qualifications, the meaning of the "Dr." title, or his business results, so we cannot comment on them. Check his own membership business as a practical proof point.

## Pros

- Focuses on recurring revenue, a model with predictable income when it works.
- Combines membership strategy with AI automation, a timely pairing.
- No paid price is listed, which lowers the barrier to evaluating it.
- 4,018 members suggests a sizable group with shared goals.
- Clearly stated outcomes: leverage and reclaimed time.

## Cons

- The AI workforce is described only in general terms.
- "Runs without you" is an ambitious claim with no supporting detail.
- No information on structure, support or member results.
- Building a membership still requires an audience and an offer, which the listing does not address.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Coaches, creators and small business owners who want to understand how to turn expertise into a recurring membership and are open to using AI to reduce workload.

## Who Should Skip It

Beginners without any audience or expertise to offer, and anyone expecting hands-off income quickly. People looking for deep technical AI engineering will not find it described here.
