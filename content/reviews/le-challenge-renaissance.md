## What's Actually Inside

The name itself, "Challenge 5 Jours" (5-Day Challenge), gives this a concrete structure that many self-improvement communities lack: a defined, time-boxed program rather than an open-ended feed. That body-focused language suggests a physical or embodiment component, though the listing doesn't specify whether that means movement, breathwork, nutrition, or something else entirely, nor does it explain what happens after the five days end.
