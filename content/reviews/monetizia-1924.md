It sits in the tech category on Skool, and the "(gratis)" tag in the display name reinforces the no-cost positioning as a core part of the brand itself.

Pairing templates with a full course is a common structure for this kind of community: the templates presumably act as a fast starting point for members who want to move quickly, while the course fills in the reasoning and steps behind them. What the listing doesn't clarify is whether the templates are specific to one type of AI offer, such as chatbot services or automation consulting, or whether they're generic enough to apply across different AI business models. That distinction matters for anyone trying to judge fit before joining.
