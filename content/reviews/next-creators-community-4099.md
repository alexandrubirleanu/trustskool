# Next Creators Community Review 2026: A $9/Month Skool Group for AI Image and Video

Nine dollars a month buys entry to Next Creators Community, an English-language Skool group with 3,005 members. Its description covers a lot in one sentence: "Master AI image and video creation, build better workflows, and turn your AI skills into real income with practical systems and real results."

## What's Actually Inside

Break the sentence into parts and the structure becomes visible. First comes creation: AI-generated images and video. Second comes process: "better workflows," which probably means repeatable ways of chaining tools together instead of one-off prompts. Third comes monetization: turning those skills "into real income."

The first two parts are concrete skills that a community can plausibly teach. The third is the one to treat cautiously. "Real income" and "real results" are strong phrases, yet the listing offers no numbers, case studies or definitions. It does not say who earned what, from which kind of work, or over what period. Skills in AI imagery and video can be sold as freelance services or used for content, but demand, competition and tool changes make earnings unpredictable.

The listing also leaves out the practical specifics: no tools are named, there is no mention of live sessions or feedback, and there is no information on how often the material is updated. In a field where generation tools change every few months, update frequency may matter more than the original lessons.

On price, $9 per month is low and sits comfortably in impulse-purchase territory. That makes it easy to try and easy to cancel, provided the cancellation process is clear. Check the current price on the live page, since the listing is a snapshot.

At 3,005 members, the community has a respectable base for a niche topic, though the data reviewed here says nothing about daily activity.

## Cons

- Tools, curriculum and update schedule are not listed.
- "Real income" and "real results" are claims without evidence in the data reviewed.
- AI creative tools change quickly, so older lessons can date fast.
- Creative output alone does not create clients or an audience, and the listing does not say how that part is handled.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Hobbyists, designers and content creators who want to improve their AI image and video work cheaply and learn from other members' workflows. It suits those who treat income as a possible side effect rather than the goal.

## Who Should Skip It

People who expect the subscription to deliver income, or who need personal coaching. If you only need the basics, free tutorials may cover the same ground.
