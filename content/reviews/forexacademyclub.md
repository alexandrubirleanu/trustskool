# Comunidade FAC Review 2026: A Free Portuguese-Language Forex Learning Community

Portuguese speakers interested in forex have fewer dedicated learning spaces than English speakers, which is part of what makes Comunidade FAC worth a look. It is a free Skool community with 3,116 members, operating in Portuguese. The welcome message, translated, reads: a free FAC community where you can find courses, classes and live webinars and learn from professional traders.

## What's Actually Inside

The description lists three content types: courses, classes ("aulas") and live webinars. That is a more concrete menu than many trading communities offer, and it points to an education-first format, with recorded lessons plus live sessions. It also says the teaching comes from "Traders Profissionais" (professional traders).

Treat that last claim carefully. The listing does not name the traders, describe their experience, or explain what "professional" means here, and no verification was available in the data reviewed. The word "gratuita" (free) is the other thing to read closely. The community is free to join, yet the name "FAC" and the phrase "the free community" suggest there may be a paid tier elsewhere. Nothing in the listing confirms that either way, so check what is included before you assume.

Forex deserves honest caution. Leveraged currency trading is high risk, and many retail participants lose money. Learning the mechanics, risk management and market structure is reasonable, but no course or webinar can promise profit, and past results shown by anyone do not predict future outcomes. If a broker is recommended during the live sessions, find out whether the community earns a commission and check that the broker is properly regulated in your country.

With 3,116 members, this is a fair-sized community for a language-specific trading group. It is enough to suggest regular activity, though the description does not state how often webinars occur.
