# DPH: Mini Course & PLR Library Review 2026: Starting a Digital Product Business

Starting a digital product business is one of those goals that sounds simple until you actually sit down to do it, and DPH: Mini Course & PLR Library is built to close that gap for total beginners. The community is free, has 6,942 members, and centers on what it calls "The Digital Product Hub Mini Course," aimed squarely at making digital products approachable for people starting from zero.

## What's Actually Inside

Two elements stand out in the description. The first is the mini course itself, a shorter, presumably more digestible format than a full multi-week program, which fits the stated goal of keeping things simple for beginners. The second is the "PLR Library" referenced in the display name, PLR standing for private label rights, meaning pre-made content or templates that members can typically customize and resell as their own without building everything from scratch. Together, that suggests the community's core promise is speed to a first product launch rather than deep, from-scratch creation skills.

Megan Roberts is the owner of this community on Skool. The description itself doesn't specify exactly which digital product types (ebooks, templates, printables, courses) the PLR library covers, so members should expect to confirm that once inside.

## Pros

- Free to join, which matters for a "beginner" audience that may not want to spend money before validating the business model.
- The PLR library angle offers a shortcut to a first product, useful for people who get stuck at the "what do I even sell" stage.
- Framed explicitly as simple and beginner-focused rather than assuming existing business experience.
- Nearly 7,000 members suggests an active audience around a fairly specific starting-out niche.

## Cons

- The description doesn't specify which digital product categories the PLR library covers, so it's unclear how directly it maps to a member's chosen niche.
- Reselling PLR content, by nature, means less differentiation from other sellers using the same materials, a limitation the description doesn't address.
- No independent reviews were found outside the platform listing itself.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Complete beginners who want a low-cost, low-complexity entry point into selling digital products and would rather adapt existing PLR materials than build something entirely original on day one.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone wanting to build a fully original, differentiated digital product brand from scratch, or more experienced sellers who have already outgrown "beginner" level content, should look for a more advanced-focused community instead.
