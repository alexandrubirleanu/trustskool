# Scalers Review 2026: Can SEO Agency Founders Really Use This to Reach 8 Figures?

Scalers is unusually precise about who it is for. The description calls it "no BS" training for SEO agency founders who want to scale to 8-figures. That rules out SEO beginners, freelancers learning keyword research, and general marketers. It is about running and growing an agency business. The community has 1,980 members and is filed in TrustSkool's money category.

The 8-figure goal is the community's own framing and should be read as an aspiration, not as a verified outcome for members.

## What's Actually Inside

The description lists four areas of "deep dives":

- **Lead generation.** How an agency finds and wins clients.
- **Delivery.** Producing SEO results for those clients at scale.
- **Operations.** Systems, hiring, and processes, presumably.
- **Mindset.** The founder side of growth.

That covers the main pressure points of agency growth, which is a good sign. Many agency communities focus only on sales. Here, delivery and operations get equal billing, which matters because scaling client work without breaking quality is where many agencies struggle.

What the listing does not describe is the format. "Training" and "deep dives" suggest recorded lessons, but there is no mention of live calls, templates, or one-to-one support.

## Pricing

The price field in the listing is empty, so by TrustSkool's data this appears to be free. The description does not mention a price either. Given the positioning around high-revenue agency founders, it is worth checking the Skool page directly in case access or upsells work differently than the listing shows. The listing also shows a 50% affiliate commission, which is high and means some recommendations you see online may come from affiliates.

## Who Runs It

Joe Davies is listed as the owner. No further background is available in the data, so this review does not assume anything about his own agency or results.

## Pros

- Very specific audience, so content should be relevant to agency founders.
- Covers delivery and operations, not just lead gen.
- Free according to the listing.
- A sizeable member base of agency owners to network with.

## Cons

- The 8-figure framing is ambitious and unverified.
- Little detail on format or how deep the training goes.
- The owner's track record is not stated in the data.
- A 50% affiliate commission means outside reviews may not be neutral.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Founders already running an SEO agency who have some clients and want to grow systematically. It should suit people who are hitting limits on lead flow, delivery capacity, or operations and want frameworks focused specifically on SEO agencies rather than generic business advice.

## Who Should Skip It

People still learning SEO itself, freelancers with no plan to build a team, and anyone running a different kind of agency who wants channel-specific training outside SEO. If you need hands-on SEO technique tutorials, this community's stated focus on business scaling suggests it is not the right place.
