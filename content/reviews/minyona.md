# MinyonaFree Review 2026: A Free SMMA Group for Contractor Marketing, or a Mastermind Funnel?

MinyonaFree speaks to a narrow audience and says so plainly. The community is aimed at SMMA (social media marketing agency) owners who serve contractors, and its description offers instant access to free trainings for succeeding in that niche. With 2,421 members, it's a mid-sized group on Skool, and joining costs nothing according to the listing.

## What's Actually Inside

The description outlines a two-step structure. First, members get free trainings focused on the contractor niche, meaning agencies that market for businesses like home services and trades. Second, members are invited to "level up" by joining a Mastermind, which the description says comes with instant course access.

That second step matters. The free community appears to function as an entry point, with the deeper course material sitting behind the Mastermind. The listing doesn't say what the Mastermind costs, what it includes beyond course access, or how much of the contractor-niche playbook is available in the free tier. The name itself, MinyonaFree, also hints that this is the free side of a larger offer.

The phrase "succeeding in the contractor niche" is the community's own framing. There are no specific results or income figures in the description, which is actually a point in its favor compared with groups that lead with revenue screenshots.

## Who Runs It

Michael Mignogna is listed as the owner. Beyond that name, no further background is available in the data, so this review doesn't make assumptions about his agency or track record.

## Pros

- Free to join, with free trainings promised immediately.
- A tightly defined niche (SMMA owners serving contractors) rather than generic agency advice.
- The upsell to a Mastermind is stated openly in the description instead of being hidden.

## Cons

- Course access is tied to the Mastermind, so the free tier may be limited by design.
- No pricing or detail is given for the Mastermind itself.
- Agencies outside the contractor space will likely find the material too specific.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

SMMA owners who already work with contractors, or are deciding whether to niche down into that market, and want to sample free training before committing money. It also fits agency owners who are comfortable evaluating a free tier first and deciding on an upgrade later.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone who dislikes communities built around a paid upgrade path should be aware that this one openly points toward a Mastermind. Marketers who serve e-commerce, SaaS, or other industries won't get much from a contractor-focused curriculum, and complete beginners with no agency yet may want broader fundamentals first.
