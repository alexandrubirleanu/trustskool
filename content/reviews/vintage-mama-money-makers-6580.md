# Vintage Mama Review 2026: Is This the Support Group Moms Over 35 Have Been Looking For?

It is free and lists 2,257 members, a solid number for such a specific audience.

## What's Actually Inside

The description is focused and warm. Two activities are named: sharing stories and sharing life hacks. That points to a peer support community where members swap experiences and practical tips rather than follow a structured program. The "one day at a time" phrasing sets a supportive, realistic tone rather than a performance-driven one.

One detail is worth flagging. The community's URL slug includes the words "money-makers," which suggests the group may once have had, or may still have, an income or side-business angle. The current description says nothing about making money, so this review does not assume the community covers that. If earning is what draws you, check inside before joining for that reason.

There is no mention of experts, health professionals, live sessions, or courses.

## Pros

## Cons

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Mothers who had children at 35 or older and want a free, friendly place to connect with women in a similar season of life, trade practical tips, and feel less alone in the day-to-day. Expecting mothers in that age range may also find it reassuring.

## Who Should Skip It

Younger parents are not the stated audience. Anyone seeking medical guidance on later-life pregnancy or parenting should rely on professionals rather than a peer group, and anyone looking primarily for income opportunities should not assume the slug reflects current content.
