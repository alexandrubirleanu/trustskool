# Built To Last Review: Free Strength Training With a Reset Protocol

Built To Last is a free English-language community with 2,668 members, and it says so up front: "This FREE community helps you gain strength for optimal health, physique and athleticism ASAP with our Reset Protocol." The last part is the hook, a named system that members are meant to follow.

## What's Actually Inside

The central item is the Reset Protocol. The listing does not explain what it involves, how long it runs or whether it covers training, nutrition, recovery or all three, so we will not guess. What we can say is that the goals are listed as strength, physique and athleticism, which points to training for both looks and performance.

Price: free, according to the description. No paid tier is shown in the data.

The word "ASAP" deserves a note. Fast results are a common promise in fitness communities, and bodies rarely cooperate on a schedule. Look for a protocol that explains progression, rest and how to scale exercises for your level, rather than one that only promises speed.

Check the classroom early. A named protocol is only useful if it is written down clearly enough to follow without a coach on the line, and if there is a way to ask questions when something does not feel right.

## Pros

- Free to join.
- A named, focused system rather than a general chat room.
- Covers strength, physique and athleticism, a combination many gym-goers want.

## Cons

- The protocol itself is not described publicly.
- Promises of speed ("ASAP") should be treated with caution.
- No stated screening for injuries or health conditions.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

People who want a free, structured route into strength training and are happy to follow a defined protocol while learning alongside other members.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone with injuries or medical conditions who needs personalised, supervised guidance, and experienced lifters wanting advanced periodisation. Speak with a doctor or qualified trainer before starting any new programme.

If you try it, treat the first couple of weeks as a trial. Record your starting numbers, follow the protocol as written, and watch how you feel, not only how the mirror looks. Pay attention to whether other members share steady, realistic progress instead of dramatic transformations. Because the community is free, you can leave without losing anything if the approach does not suit your body or goals.
