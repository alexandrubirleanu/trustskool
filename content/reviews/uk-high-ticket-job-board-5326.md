# UK High Ticket Job Board Review 2026: Free Job Board for High Ticket Sales Roles

The UK High Ticket Job Board is a free community with about 3,930 members, and its description calls it the UK job board for high ticket closers and setters looking to find real roles, run by a trusted sales agency in the UK. It also invites members to join, network, learn and add value.

## What's Actually Inside

Three activities are named: finding roles, networking and learning. The job board is the core purpose. The listing does not say how many openings are posted, how often they are refreshed, which industries the roles are in, or what the commission structure usually looks like. We make no claims about specific employers or pay.

## Pros

- Free to join, which matters for people early in a sales career who are not yet earning.
- A clear and unusual focus on UK roles for closers and setters instead of general advice.
- Close to 4,000 members gives a sizeable network of people in the same field.

## Cons

- The number and quality of job postings are not stated, so the board's usefulness is unproven.
- The "trusted" claim is unverifiable from the listing.
- High ticket sales roles are often commission based. The listing does not discuss pay, so read any offer carefully and ask about terms.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

UK-based people who want to start or move within high ticket sales as a closer or setter and would like a free place to look for openings and meet others in the field.

## Who Should Skip It

Anyone outside the UK, or anyone who is not interested in remote or commission-style sales roles, will get little from it. Readers who want structured sales training should look for a community that documents a curriculum, since this listing does not.
