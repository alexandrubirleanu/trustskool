## Process notes

- No external research (WebFetch/WebSearch) was used anywhere in this run. Every claim in every review comes only from the fields in `data/communities.json` (description, display_name, owner_name, price fields, total_members, language). No founder biography was invented for any community; where `owner_name` was present, it was stated plainly as the community's listed owner on Skool, with no additional background beyond the bare name.
- Work was split across 10 parallel batches of 10 candidate communities each, ordered by `total_members` descending. Each batch was independently instructed on tone, rules, and format, then the full output was re-verified programmatically against every non-negotiable rule (word count match, em dash absence, exact CTA format, slug/frontmatter consistency, no duplicate files).
- One cosmetic near-miss was found and accepted during validation: `ailaunch`'s `display_name` in the source data ends with a trailing space before the trailing emoji. The writing agent dropped that trailing space before the possessive `'s` in the CTA line, which is the correct, readable rendering rather than a rule violation.
